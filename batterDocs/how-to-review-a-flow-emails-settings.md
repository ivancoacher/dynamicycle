<h1>How to review a flow email’s settings</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to view and configure the settings for a flow email which includes settings such as Smart Sending and UTM tracking.

## Review a flow email’s settings

1. Click on an email card within a flow.
2. View the ****Settings**** section at the bottom of the righthand sidebar. You'll see the following settings:
   - ****Skip recently emailed profiles****Decide whether you would like to turn [Smart Sending](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002779311-Smart-Sending-for-Flows) on or off for a particular email, which skips recently emailed profiles.
   - ****Enable UTM Tracking****
     Decide whether you would like to turn on [UTM Tracking](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005247808-UTM-Tracking-in-Klaviyo) for a particular email.
   - ****Additional filters****
     Add [additional filters](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002779091-Add-an-Additional-Filter-to-a-Single-Flow-Email) that apply exclusively to a particular email. This can be a useful tool if you would like to restrict an email to a particular group of people without creating an entirely separate path for them to go down using a conditional split.
