<h1>How to set up multi-store syndication for reviews</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to share reviews between 2 or more of your storefronts, also called multi-store syndication. This is helpful if you have multiple storefronts for different locations, but sell some or all of the same products in each.

## Requirements

In order to configure multi-store syndication, all accounts must be part of the same portfolio. [Set up a portfolio](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/24656917054747) containing all the accounts you’d like to syndicate before proceeding. A Klaviyo account can only be a part of one syndication group, but a portfolio may contain multiple groups. Adding an account to a syndication group will remove it from any other syndication group it is currently part of.

A maximum of 10 Klaviyo accounts can be syndicated at one time.

## About syndicating reviews to other stores

Multi-store syndication allows you to share reviews across products hosted on different storefronts. Syndication is two-way, meaning all reviews from every syndicated account will appear in reviews widgets for all other syndicated accounts. Reviews will be ordered based on each account’s [language and translation settings](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/28858764961947).

Reviews are mapped between accounts based on unique SKUs. This means every store you intend to syndicate should use the same SKUs for matching products.

Reviews must be [moderated](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/19351110471323) from the account where they were originally submitted. You cannot approve a review in one syndicated account, but reject it in another. Reviews will only appear in your Klaviyo reviews dashboard in the account where the review originated.

## Enable review syndication

After completing this process, reviews will begin appearing across all selected storefronts within 1 week.

1. From an account you’d like to syndicate, navigate to ****Reviews****.
2. Select ****Reviews settings****.
3. Select ****Syndication****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/33625718938139)
4. In the **Multi-store** section, toggle on the setting ****Syndicate reviews to other stores****.
5. Select the account(s) that should share reviews with the one you’re currently logged in to. You can only syndicate reviews between accounts where you are an owner or an admin; if you don’t have sufficient access, ask your Klaviyo account administrator to upgrade your access in all accounts you’d like to syndicate.
6. Click ****Save changes****.

## Disable review syndication

If you’d like to remove syndication after setting it up, there are 2 options:

- Remove a single store from a syndicated group
- Disable syndication for the entire group

You must be an admin or owner for every account in the syndicated group in order to disable syndication entirely.

To remove a single store from a syndicated group:

1. Navigate to a different Klaviyo account within the syndicated group. This process cannot be completed within the account you are removing.
2. Navigate to ****Reviews****.
3. Select ****Reviews settings****.
4. Select ****Syndication****.
5. In the **Multi-store** section, deselect the account you’d like to remove from the syndicated group.
6. Click ****Save changes****.

   To disable syndication for an entire group:
7. Navigate to ****Reviews**** from any account in the syndication group.
8. Select ****Reviews settings****.
9. Select ****Syndication****.
10. In the **Multi-store** section, toggle off the setting ****Syndicate reviews to other stores****.
11. Click ****Save changes****.

## Outcome

Once you enable multi-store syndication, reviews will begin appearing across all selected storefronts within 1 week.
