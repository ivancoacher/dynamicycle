<h1>How to reset your Klaviyo password</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to reset a forgotten Klaviyo password when you're unable to log in.

## Before you begin

This guide will be useful for those who are locked out of their Klaviyo accounts due to a forgotten password. Review the following scenarios to determine if an alternative solution may apply to your situation:

- Still have access but want to change password
  - If you are able to log into your account but want to change your password or update your account email, please refer to our guide on changing your account email or password in Klaviyo.
- Lost access to account email
  - If you've lost access to the email you previously used for your Klaviyo account, contact the account's Owner or Admin to have [your new email address added as a user](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053547071).
    - If you are the only user on the account, please [submit a request here](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) for help.

## Reset a forgotten password

If you have forgotten your password, you must reset it via the Klaviyo login page.

1. Navigate to the [Klaviyo login page](https://www.klaviyo.com/login).
2. Select ****Forgot Your Password?****
   ![The Klaviyo login page where you enter your account's email and password.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29406340853403)
3. Input the email address associated with your account.
   ![The Forgot your password modal showing where you can enter the email address associated with your account to send a password reset email.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29406296013339)
4. Select ****Send email****.
   ![An Email Sent confirmation message that shows when you enter your email address in the Forgot your password modal.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29406296017563)

   You will have 3 hours to use the password reset link after receiving it.
5. Open your email account and locate the "Reset your Klaviyo password" email. Be sure to check your inbox, spam, and promotions folder for this message.
6. Click on the password reset link in the email or paste it into your browser.
7. On the **Reset Password** modal, create a new password to regain access to your account. Your new password must meet the following criteria:

   - At least 12 characters long![The Reset password modal where you can enter a new password for your account, then retype it, then save it.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29406296019483)
8. Select ****Reset password**** to save this change.

Next, navigate back to the Klaviyo login page and log in again with your new password.

## Still unable to log into your Klaviyo account?

If you do not see a password reset email in your inbox, please check your spam and promotional tabs. Occasionally these emails may end up there.

If you've waited a few moments and still do not see the link in any of your email tabs, please [submit a request](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) for further assistance. From there, a member of our Support Team will reach out to assist in retrieving your access.
