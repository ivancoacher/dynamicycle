<h1>Understanding third-party apps</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to find third-party apps, which are apps built by third parties (instead of by Klaviyo) using Klaviyo's APIs. Klaviyo's APIs enable a large variety of third-parties to build apps and integrations with our platform. You can search for both Klaviyo-built integrations and select third-party apps in our [app marketplace](https://marketplace.klaviyo.com/en-us/). If you are looking for an app you can't find on our marketplace, we recommend reaching out to the platform directly.

## Find a third-party app

To find a third party app:

1. In Klaviyo, select the ****Integrations**** tab.
2. Click ****Explore apps****.
3. You'll be brought to our app marketplace where you can browse and search for apps.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39451226896539)
4. Select an app to learn more about it.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39451202479003)

## Where to find help resources

All Klaviyo-built (or "native") integrations have help documentation on our Help Center, while third-party apps do not. You can find integration guides for Klaviyo-built integrations in the [Integrations section](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/categories/115000032731-Ecommerce-Integrations). For third-party apps, documentation is linked on the individual app's page in our marketplace under **Help resources**.
