<h1>Getting started with Advanced KDP</h1>

## You will learn

Learn more about using Advanced KDP (Klaviyo data platform) to gather robust customer insights across all your marketing initiatives. Advanced KDP enables more advanced data management, manipulation, and analysis. It also provides actionable strategies to employ across each of your individual audiences and your business as a whole.

## Before you begin

Advanced KDP is separate from the email and profile plans, and you must have both to access the functionality. Head to our [billing guide](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000976672) to learn more about this plan or [get started](https://www.klaviyo.com/customer-data-platform) if you are a new customer.

Advanced KDP is built on the same infrastructure as Klaviyo’s marketing application, and it allows you to more effectively manage and activate data, run advanced reporting and predictive modeling, and sync data in Klaviyo with other systems at scale.

## What is a Advanced KDP?

A customer data platform is a system that collects and unifies data from multiple sources at scale, and makes it available for manipulation and distribution to other analytics, insights, marketing automation, and ad platforms.

Klaviyo’s Advanced KDP is a fully integrated customer data platform that enables more effective management and activation of your data. Klaviyo’s Advanced KDP provides tools to enrich and transform data, run more advanced reporting and predictive modeling, and sync data from Klaviyo to other outside systems.

## Purchasing the Advanced KDP

Advanced KDP is not included in Klaviyo’s standard marketing application, and a subscription is required to access the associated functionality.

### New Klaviyo customers

If you are a new customer, you can purchase Klaviyo’s Advanced KDP, along with an email or SMS plan. Learn more about [Klaviyo plans and Advanced KDP](http://klaviyo.com/customer-data-platform).

### Existing Klaviyo customers

To purchase Advanced KDP:

1. Navigate to your account in the bottom left corner and select ****Billing****.
2. Select the ****Change plan**** button to proceed to checkout, where you'll see the option to add an Advanced KDP plan.
3. Select Advanced KDP on the checkout page and enter the number of profiles you need for your plan.
4. Select the ****Continue to payment****to checkout and complete your purchase.

![CDP purchase option at checkout](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720897725851)

Within billing, Advanced KDP is called **CDP**.

### Billing

Since Advanced KDP allows you to better understand all customers (not just the ones you send to), the plans are based on total profiles in your account. The overall total profile count is the total number of profiles associated with your Klaviyo account, regardless of their marketing status and channel (this includes email, SMS, and push profiles today). Any profile that is being stored and tracked in Klaviyo will be represented in this count.

Note that it is best practice to retain all your profile data with Advanced KDP, and suppress rather than delete inactive profiles. This allows the Advanced KDP to leverage all your customer data when creating predictions, and provide more reliable reports and insights.

Additionally, deleted profiles can enter your Klaviyo account again as a new profile through engagement with your brand, and trigger unwanted marketing automations. Similarly, email addresses that may get added by a bot as part of a [list bombing](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/9890182538011) attack can be added again, potentially harming your deliverability.

Learn more about [Advanced KDP billing](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000976672).

### Intelligence vs. data management

The features available as part of Advanced KDP are split into 2 groups:

- ****Intelligence****
  The **Intelligence** components of Advanced KDP allow you to leverage Klaviyo for your reporting needs beyond marketing channel performance, focusing on strategic audience and business intelligence. Within **Intelligence**, features are organized into 3 groups:
  - Customer insights
  - Catalog insights
  - Predictive models

- ****Data management**** The **Data management** components of Advanced KDP allow you to manipulate and transform your data to make it more actionable, with better functionality to support getting data in and out of Klaviyo.

After purchasing the Advanced KDP, you’ll see the navigation menu in Klaviyo update with an additional item called **Advanced KDP**.

Within the **Advanced KDP** item in Klaviyo navigation, you’ll see the following tabs to access the new features:

- ****Intelligence****
- **Data management**

![Compare distribution of customers in different groups](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720897719067)

These groupings enable you to analyze customer behavioral patterns over time and identify areas where you can optimize your marketing strategies.

For those customers who may be at risk, you can also use these insights to drive winback campaigns and reduce churn.

### Funnel analysis report

The [funnel analysis report](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/17798009376155) provides the ability to see where customers may be dropping off in their journey with your brand. The report allows you to set up and analyze the specific steps of a customer’s marketing journey and the number of customers who reach each step.

For example, you may create an analysis that starts with customers who **Placed Order**, then **Fulfilled Order**, and finally **Refunded Order,** as shown below.

Within this analysis, you can review a breakdown of the number and percentage of customers who reached each step and see where you can optimize your marketing efforts.

### Catalog insights

The [product analysis dashboard](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/26685770823451) in Advanced KDP allows you better understand customer behaviors related to specific product purchases, and craft marketing to match these pathways.

You can review insights around how, what, and when products in your catalog are driving orders, and align products at the right time in the customer journey.

Some methods you can strategically use product analysis insights include:

- ****Adjust post-purchase flow timing****Set up a flow triggered off of a purchase event and add a wait step based on insights from the [Repeat purchase timing card](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/26685770823451#h_01J0NVG9HWRA103QJGCPCDHZK6).

- ****Adjust post-purchase flow content****Set up a flow triggered off of a purchase event to promote a product recommendation from insights in the [Products bought in the next order card](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/26685770823451#h_01J0NVG9HWSK1BQ0Q1AQ50176Z).

- ****Automate recommended bundles/co-purchases****Set up a flow triggered off of an abandoned cart event with a reminder to purchase and a product recommendation from [Products bought in the same cart card](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/26685770823451#h_01J0NVG9HWPA5VBCCQH5R90SYC).

- ****Inform your merchandising strategy****Identify the best products to promote together or in succession in campaigns based on [Products bought in the next order](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/26685770823451#h_01J0NVG9HWSK1BQ0Q1AQ50176Z) or [Products bought in same cart](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/26685770823451#h_01J0NVG9HWPA5VBCCQH5R90SYC) cards.

![Product analysis dashboard showing repeat purchase timing](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/30160522258971)

Additionally, the product analysis dashboard creates 2 predictive profile properties in the **Predictive Analytics** section of a profile. These properties are:

- ****Best cross-sell date****The product analysis report determines the best timing to attempt to cross-sell to profiles. The **Best cross-sell date** property requires customers to have made at least 1 purchase, and is based on the purchasing patterns of similar customers that have bought the same item. This is a dynamic property that updates daily, but only when a profile makes a purchase.
- ****Next best product****The product analysis report also determines the next best product for profiles based on their last purchase. As a profile continues to place orders, this property will automatically update accordingly. The **Next best product** automatically excludes unavailable items, as well as the first 48 hours of repeat purchase data for a profile. The first 48 hours after a purchase generally has the highest volume of returns and exchanges. Excluding data captured during this timeframe prevents the **Next best product** property from being skewed.

  You can take advantage of this profile property in email sends through the **Next best product** block. This product block will dynamically show each recipient the next best product they are most likely to purchase based on their last purchase.

Learn more about [how to use these properties to build dynamic cross-sell flows](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33789205940507).

### Customizable CLV report

The [customer lifetime value (CLV) report](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/17797865070235) predicts your customers’ expected purchases and behaviors over time. This report details your customers' current buying habits across each of your marketing channels and functions (campaigns, flows, forms, and segments), and then models potential future purchases based on this data.

These insights provide opportunities for you to cross-sell and up-sell upcoming purchases, as well as how to best personalize the customer buying experience, including around key times of year and events that matter to certain customers. Examples of some of this report’s features are shown below.

## Data management features

### Data warehouse sync

The [data warehouse syncing](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/17759932376475) feature allows you to sync profile and event data to third-party data warehouses at a regular cadence, allowing you to store and analyze key information about your customers outside of Klaviyo.

Klaviyo supports connections with the following data warehouses:

- Amazon Redshift
- Amazon S3
- Google BigQuery
- Microsoft Azure
- Snowflake

### Webhooks

[Webhooks](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/17760478970907) in Klaviyo enable you to build webhook-based applications in external systems based on events that are captured in Klaviyo. They can pass information or “call” other applications, tools, and servers via HTTP POST requests to notify your external systems that an event has occurred.

Webhooks available with Advanced KDP support message-related events like **Unsubscribed**, **Received email**, or **Clicked email**. You can also set up webhooks without the limitations of being in a [flow](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4534329515931).

![Create webhook modal](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720897716763)

### Data transformation

The [data transformation](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/17760400736539) tool in Klaviyo enables you to transform profile properties in Klaviyo without the need for development.

You can **Format**, **Standardize,** or **Merge** your profile property values with this tool, allowing you to make your data more useful and for more accurate segmentation and personalization.

With the **Format** transformation method, you can update profile property values’ formatting.

![Format transformation options](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720897748891)

With the **Standardization** method you can set rules to automatically replace specific profile property values.

![Standardize transformation method](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720897706395)

With the **Merge** transformation method, you can merge multiple custom profile properties into 1 property.

![Example merge transformation](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720903057947)

### Code

[Code](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/18620644491035) enables you to execute custom functions in response to events captured in Klaviyo. You can write your own Javascript or Python code in the editor, and they are then executed directly in the platform.

With Code, you can send data to your external systems without having to set up a public HTTP endpoint to receive webhooks, and build custom functionality that is triggered in response to an event’s occurrence. You can also access many popular prebuilt modules to facilitate creating custom solutions.

![Code editor for a javascript function](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720897728795)

Learn [how to build custom functions using Code](https://academy.klaviyo.com/build-custom-functions-with-code).

### Custom monitors

With [custom monitors](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/27160071187739), you can configure alerts to quickly identify and resolve issues around key metrics and objects in Klaviyo (i.e., segments and flows).

There are 3 types of custom monitors you can set up in Klaviyo:

- ****Single flow monitor****
  A flow to monitor key performance metrics for and receive alerts about.
- ****Single metric monitor****
  An event to monitor and receive alerts about.
- ****Single segment monitor****A segment to monitor and receive alerts about.

![Example alert rule with conditions that must be met to trigger alert](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720903062939)

## Other benefits

### Custom metrics

While custom metrics are available on all Klaviyo accounts, purchasing Advanced KDP increases the number of custom metrics permitted on your account from 1 to 50.

The custom metrics feature in Klaviyo allows you to configure your own events for more accurate reporting and events that better represent your business operations.

By default, Klaviyo provides metrics that track your business’s performance and measure success (e.g., a Placed Order event). However, there may be instances where you need to create custom metrics to more accurately capture how your business runs. For example, your Placed Order events include recurring subscriptions by default, and you’d like to review only orders placed through your ecommerce website.

Learn [how to configure custom metrics](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/22311085738395) in Klaviyo.

Some example of common use cases for custom metrics include:

- ****Separating retail and ecommerce events (Shopify or other custom integrations)****With Shopify and some custom integrations, physical retail (POS) and e-commerce orders will come through as a single event (e.g., Shopify Placed Order event).

  However, if you would like to separate these out to see the efficacy of each individual event type, you can set up a custom metric like the example below. Here, the point of sale (POS) is not being included in the Placed Order event and combined with other ecommerce data.
  ![Placed orders that are not through a POS system](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720897737243)
- ****Removing recurring subscription events with Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce****As noted above, standard Shopify integrations, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce will include subscriptions in Placed Order events automatically. This means that they are calculating both ecommerce and subscription orders together. In the example below, by using does not equal, you can isolate and review Placed Order metrics that do not include subscription orders.
  ![Placed orders that are not from recurring subscriptions](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720903056283)

  Learn more about [how to use custom conversion metrics and see more examples of common use cases](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/22311212640027).
