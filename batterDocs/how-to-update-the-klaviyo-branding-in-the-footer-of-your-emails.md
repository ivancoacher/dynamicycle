<h1>How to update the Klaviyo branding in the footer of your emails</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to customize the footer of your emails to best suit your business needs.

Please note that by default, all free email accounts include Klaviyo branding in their email footers. However, all customers on [paid profile and email plans](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050759151) will automatically have Klaviyo's branding removed.

This guide addresses how to adjust the default Klaviyo branding in email templates. For assistance with editing the content in your templates, head to our [Guide to the email template editor](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407911841435).

## Update branding in your email footers

For free accounts, the Klaviyo logo will appear at the bottom of all emails.

![Klaviyo logo footer.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717849899163)

To choose a different style for your default Klaviyo branding:

1. Click on your organization name in the bottom left corner of Klaviyo.
2. Click ****Settings****.
   ![The Organization name selected in the bottom left corner showing a menu of settings options.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31422153062171)
3. Select the ****Email**** tab.
4. In the **Sender information** section, select 1 of 4 options to tailor the Klaviyo branding to your preferred style.
5. To apply your chosen style, click ****Save****.
   ![The Sender Information menu on the Email settings page showing the 4 different default branding options of the Klaviyo logo.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31422153066523)

For more guidance on upgrading or changing your account's plan, see [How to change your plan in Klaviyo](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/8356575957275).
