<h1>How to display recently viewed items in an email</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to include a reminder of the items someone has recently viewed or added to their cart in an email.

## Before you begin

Before taking the following steps, make sure:

- You’ve synced your product catalog with Klaviyo. Most of Klaviyo’s ecommerce integrations (such as Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, etc.) [sync your catalog automatically](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005082787-How-to-use-product-feeds-and-recommendations).
- You have [Viewed Product tracking enabled](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4416172774939-Troubleshooting-Viewed-Product-tracking#before-you-begin1).

## Create a personalized product feed

To create a product feed that displays someone’s recently viewed products:

1. From Klaviyo’s sidebar, click ****Content > Products****.
2. Click ****Manage Product Feeds****. ****![The Manage Product Feeds option in Klaviyo](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720668630683)****
3. Click ****Create Product Feed****.
4. Name the product feed something descriptive, like RECENTLY\_VIEWED\_PRODUCTS. Note that spaces are not permitted in feed names.
5. In the **What products should the customers view first?** dropdown, scroll down and click ****Products a customer has recently viewed****. Alternatively, select ****Products a customer has added to cart**** for a feed of items they've recently added to their cart.  ****![The settings for a product feed of items a recipient recently viewed](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720668636059)****
6. Complete the additional fields as desired.
7. Click ****Save Product Feed****.

## Add viewed items to an email

Once you’ve created your product feed, navigate to an email template. You can add recently viewed products to any email template (including those in campaigns and flows). Once you’ve opened the editor for the template you’d like to work with:

1. Drag a ****Product**** block into your template.
   ![The product block icon in the Klaviyo template editor](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720656835099)
2. In the **Content** settings, make sure the feed is set to ****Dynamic****, then choose the feed you created in the **Product feed** field.
   ![A product block with the recently viewed product feed selected](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720668641691)
3. Modify the other options in the **Styles** tab to adjust how your viewed products appear, including how many products to display.
4. When you’re finished, click ****Done**** and save your template.

## Outcome

When this message is sent, recipients will see the products they viewed (or added to cart) most recently in the product block section of the email. If a recipient has a limited history of viewed products, they’ll see bestselling or most trending products, depending on the options selected in your feed settings.

Note that Klaviyo is only able to track viewed items for [cookied users](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005076767-Guide-to-Klaviyo-Onsite-Tracking#who-klaviyo-tracks5).
