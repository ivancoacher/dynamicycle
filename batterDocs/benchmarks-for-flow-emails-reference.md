<h1>Benchmarks for flow emails reference</h1>

## You will learn

Learn about benchmarks that you can use to compare your flow's performance with those of other Klaviyo customers. After you create a new flow, you may have questions around how it is performing.  We've aggregated data across thousands of customers in different revenue bands to give you insight into areas that are performing well, as well as where you can improve. For tips, check out the guides in the [Additional resources](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033669452#additional-resources13) section.

All the tables below reference AOV (average order value). If you are unsure of what your average order value is, [check out our guide to calculating average order value](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000676712-Calculating-Your-Average-Order-Value-AOV-) first.
