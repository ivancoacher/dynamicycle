<h1>How to show product ratings in emails with a product block</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to showcase product ratings in a product block within a campaign or flow email.

Showing the rating of your featured products in emails highlights product quality and customer satisfaction which can increase click-through rates and conversions.

Ratings will display as a star symbol with a number out of 5 next to it. The amount of reviews will appear in parentheses if this option is selected. The font style can be adjusted in the email template editor.

## Before you begin

This option to show ratings within a product block is only available in accounts that use Klaviyo Reviews. Learn how to [get started with Klaviyo Reviews](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/15937542819355).

If you haven’t already, learn the basics of product blocks, and [how to add a product block to an email](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000219092).

## Ratings in a product block vs a review quote block

Ratings within a product block allow you to show the average rating and amount of reviews for multiple products. This is different from showing a single product and specific text review using a review quote block. Learn [how to feature reviews in emails with the review quote block](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/18007373861915).

## Turn on ratings in a product block

1. Navigate to any email in Klaviyo (i.e., a flow email, draft campaign email, or email template).
2. Drag and drop a product block into the template or click an existing product block.

   - If you created a new product block, select either ****Dynamic**** or ****Static**** and choose a product feed or specific products respectively.
3. In the **Product details** section of the sidebar, check the box for ****Rating**** to show the star rating for each product in the block.

- Optional: Check the box for ****Number of ratings**** to show how many customers submitted a rating for the product.

![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/32781470498843)

## Change the appearance of the ratings

After turning on ratings within a product block, you can change the following elements of the rating’s appearance:

- Font
- Size
- Color
- Letter spacing
- Top padding
- Bottom padding
- Styling (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough)

To modify the appearance of the rating:

1. Click on the product block.
2. In the sidebar, scroll down to the **Text** section.
3. Click ****Rating****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/32781490401179)
4. Change the appearance to however you’d like.

The color and size of the star is based on the text color and size. The star shape can’t be changed.
