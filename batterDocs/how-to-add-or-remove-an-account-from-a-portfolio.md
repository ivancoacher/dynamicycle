<h1>How to add or remove an account from a portfolio</h1>

You must be a portfolio Owner and also an Owner or Admin in the account(s) that you want to add or remove.

## You will learn

Learn how to add or remove an account from an existing portfolio in Klaviyo.

This article is only for those with an existing portfolio. If you do not have a portfolio, see [how to set up a portfolio](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/24656917054747).

## Add an account to a portfolio

1. Log in to your portfolio account.
2. Navigate to ****Manage > Accounts****.
   ![Navigation menu for a portfolio account](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28711731645467)
3. Select ****Add accounts**** in the upper right.
4. Check the boxes for the account(s) you want to add.
   Note: you must be an Owner or Admin in the account you want to add. Any account that you are a user in (but not an Owner or Admin) shows under the **Unavailable** tab.
   ![Selecting accounts to link to a portfolio](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28711702669083)
5. Select ****Add****.

## Remove or delete an account from a portfolio

You can remove an account from a portfolio from either the portfolio or through the account directly.

### Portfolio instructions

Only the Owner can remove an account from within the portfolio. To do so:

1. Log in to your portfolio account.
2. Navigate to ****Manage > Accounts****.
   ![Accounts tab, where you can see every account linked to a portfolio](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28711731645467)
3. Search for the account you want to remove.
4. Click the 3-dot menu next to that account.
5. Click ****Remove from portfolio****.
   ![Menu where you can choose to remove an account from a portfolio](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28711702644635)
6. Confirm by clicking ****Remove**** in the modal.
   ![Modal to confirm that you want to unlink an account from the portfolio](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28711702649371)

### Account instructions

From within an account, any Owner or Admin in the account can remove it from a portfolio:

1. Log in to that account.
2. Click the account name on the bottom left.
3. Navigate to ****Settings > Organization****.
4. Select ****Remove from portfolio****.
   ![Organization tab showing the option to unlink the account from a portfolio](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28711702655643)
5. Click ****Remove**** in the modal.
   ![Modal to confirm that you want to unlink an account from the portfolio](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28711702662427)
