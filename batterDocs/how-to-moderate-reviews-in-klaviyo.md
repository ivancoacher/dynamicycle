<h1>How to moderate reviews in Klaviyo</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to moderate product and store reviews in Klaviyo. Moderating allows you to publish all valid reviews while rejecting inappropriate or irrelevant reviews (e.g., reviews with offensive content).

## Before you begin

Customer reviews, including their collection and management, are governed by various regulators. [Learn recommended best practices](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/16685026123035) for enabling transparency, fairness, and compliance throughout your reviews program.

## Set auto-approval settings

1. Select the ****Reviews**** tab in Klaviyo.
2. Click ****Reviews settings****.
3. Select ****Publishing rules and notifications****.
   ![Publishing rules and notifications settings](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720761655963)
4. Choose your auto-approval settings:
   1. Whether to auto-approve reviews.
   2. Which star ratings to auto-approve (e.g., approve all 4- and 5-star reviews, but require manual review for all others).
   3. Whether reviews with media (i.e., images) or profanity require manual approval.
   4. Whether reviews containing certain keywords (e.g., top-level domains like “.com”) should be flagged for manual approval to catch potential spam or links.
5. Click ****Save changes****.

![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/36274349748123)

## Moderate reviews

1. Select the ****Reviews**** tab in Klaviyo.
2. Click ****All reviews****. Here, you’ll see a list of all reviews.
3. Select ****Pending**** to only see reviews that require moderation.

   You can also filter reviews by time period, rating, reply (i.e., **Includes reply** or **No reply**), and whether a review is verified.
   ![Pending reviews](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720761662619)
   ![Reviews moderation additional filters](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720773413659)
4. You have 2 options for every pending review:
   1. Click ****Publish**** to publish the review publicly, or
   2. Open the ****Reject**** menu and select a rejection reason.

      Use discretion when rejecting reviews. For compliance purposes, it’s strongly recommended that you publish all reviews unless they meet one of the following criteria: **Profanity/Inappropriate**, **Contains private information**, **Unrelated to the product or service**, **False or misleading**, or **Fake**. You can also select **Other** if the reason doesn’t fall into one of the options above. When rejecting a review without text, you must provide a reason in the open text field. See our guide on [best practices for collecting reviews](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/16685026123035) for more details on when it is acceptable to reject a review.
5. Repeat the process with all pending reviews. Periodically review and publish or reject any with the **Pending** status (e.g., once per week).

   Review authors are not notified if their review is rejected.

   If auto-publish is turned on, any verified review with a **Pending** status is automatically published after 14 days to ensure accuracy and transparency. Learn about [reviews regulations and best practices](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/16685026123035).

   If you have a large number of pending reviews and want to publish them all at once, you can do this from the ****Overview**** tab.

   To publish all pending reviews at once:
6. Navigate to ****Reviews**** in Klaviyo.
7. In the **Overview** tab, scroll to the **Pending activity** card.
8. Open the additional options (3 dots) menu.
9. Select ****Publish all pending reviews****.
   ![The option to publish all pending reviews](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29565044594203)
10. In the modal that appears, confirm that you’re ready to publish all pending reviews.

### Moderation and reviews without text

Many countries have [regulations governing review moderation](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/16685026123035) which typically require brands to:

- Publish all legitimate reviews.
- Reject reviews only if they meet a specific set of criteria, such as:
  - Containing offensive language
  - Including private information

Exercise caution when rejecting reviews, especially those without text, to ensure compliance with these regulations.

If a review without text doesn’t meet your auto-approval settings, it will be marked as **Pending** for 14 days, or until you publish it. If you are able to resolve the customer’s complaint during this period (e.g., through a [customer service flow](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/16680027976731)) and the customer updates their review, only the updated review will be published.

### Moderate images

When moderating reviews with images, you have the following options:

- ****Rotate****
  If the image is uploaded in the wrong orientation, rotate it so it appears correctly in reviews widgets.
- ****Hide****
  If an image is inappropriate or contains sensitive information, hide it so only the review text appears in reviews widgets.

To moderate a review’s image:

1. Navigate to ****Reviews > All reviews****.
2. In the filter sidebar, select ****Photos**** to filter for reviews that include an image.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39490614495899)
3. Click the image you'd like to adjust.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39490614499355)
4. In the modal that appears, select ****Hide**** to hide the image anywhere the review appears publicly or ****Rotate**** to rotate the image counter-clockwise.
