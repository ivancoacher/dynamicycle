<h1>How to request a premium success and support package</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to request a different support and success package. Klaviyo offers 3 tiers for support and success:

- Standard
- Professional
- Enterprise

Want to learn more about what’s included in the different tiers? Contact your Klaviyo support representative.

## Before you begin

Note that the professional and enterprise support and success plans are only available for accounts paying above a certain amount per month. See the table below for eligibility.

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ****Total monthly plan cost**** | ****Standard**** | ****Professional**** | ****Enterprise**** |
| Less than $5,000 | ✔ | ✖ | ✖ |
| $5,000-$9,999 | ✔ | ✔ | ✖ |
| $10,000 or more | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |

## Request a different support plan

1. Select your organization’s name in the bottom left corner.
2. Click ****Billing > Change plan****.
3. Choose either ****Current Plan**** or ****Choose Plan**** to either keep your current plan or upgrade, respectively.
   ![The top of the billing overview page](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723662081947)
4. In the **Success and support packages** section, click ****Edit****.
   Note that this section will not appear if you are not eligible for the professional or enterprise plans.
   ![Checkout page showing the success and support packages section](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723662096283)
5. Choose an eligible plan.
   ![Dropdown for all success and support packages](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723662090779)
6. Click ****Confirm**** to finish****.****
