<h1>Edit Recharge Subscriptions with Klaviyo Helpdesk</h1>

## Why this matters

Every minute you spend jumping between Klaviyo, Recharge, and Shopify is a minute you could spend growing sales. The ****Edit Subscription**** modal lets you cancel, skip, or change a customer’s subscription right from the ticket—no extra tabs, no re-typing data, and far fewer mistakes.

Typical wins for small stores
• Skip next month’s shipment for a holiday without losing the customer forever
• Add an extra item quantity before the order ships, saving a reship fee

## 30-Second Quick Action

1. Open the customer’s Helpdesk ticket
2. Click ****Subscription**** in the right-hand panel
3. Click the tri-tip selector and click ****Cancel Subscription**** or ****Edit Subscription****
4. Make your change (edit quantity, skip, cancel)

## Before You Start (1-Minute Check)

✓ Recharge is already integrated with Klaviyo
✓ You’re logged in as an Owner or Admin (you already have permission)
✓ The order is less than 60 days old and not fully shipped

Estimated time: 3–5 minutes per change

## Step-by-Step

1. Open ****Helpdesk**** > click the customer’s ticket.
2. On the right, find the ****Subscription**** card and select ****Edit subscription****.
   • If the button is greyed out, hover for the reason (usually “order fully fulfilled”).
3. In the ****Items**** tab
   • Use ****+ / –**** for quantity changes.
4. Go to ****Status****
   • Choose ****Skip**** (moves renewal forward one cycle) or ****Cancel**** (stops it).
   • Pro tip: Use ****Skip**** when the customer just needs a short break—keeps revenue and churn low.
5. Click ****Save changes****. A green confirmation banner appears and Klaviyo posts a note to the conversation.

## FAQ

Q: How long do I have to undo?
A: 5 minutes after you hit Save.

Q: Can I issue a refund here?
A: Not yet—do that in Shopify or Recharge.

Q: Does skipping keep my analytics clean?
A: Yes. Recharge shifts the renewal date, so you keep the subscriber without skewing churn.
