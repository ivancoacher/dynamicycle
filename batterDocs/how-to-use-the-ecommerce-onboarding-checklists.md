<h1>How to use the ecommerce onboarding checklists</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to set up a customized ecommerce onboarding checklist and get started with Klaviyo.

## Access and download the checklists

When you attempt to access one of the ecommerce checklists, it will first open in Google drive and be in view-only mode.

1. Choose your applicable ecommerce integration or SMS checklist from the list below.

- [BigCommerce onboarding checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15UJnUUb2iK6x_Oo2pD0TLaxYOGP2V342YmohDysJbLg/edit?usp=sharing)

- [Magento 2 onboarding checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1RfyG5atAC15HXTw2WrR1KSvg9bbtCl_DjD3DF4G7Mus/edit?usp=sharing)
- [OpenCart onboarding checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1m_JSwLmFA7-ovZHeDhG48eiKjKL3igC5qdmSt1Krd48/edit?usp=sharing)
- [PrestaShop onboarding checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1W1xxoSLXeWSMORZZqkEfCD0gJU2AEj5etkv0W7IsmIw/edit?usp=sharing)
- [Salesforce Commerce Cloud onboarding checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VO3I1w_iXWPaJsbo3rcPGirr6-CGp9ZYebRIAeMUJac/edit?usp=sharing)
- [Shopify onboarding checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/18jP3G-8vVml1t3GPI1o5bhd6EVbiGzIzirVMKIKlT_w/edit?usp=sharing)

2. To make your own editable copy, go to ****File > Make a Copy****.
3. If you want to edit the checklist in Excel, select  ****File > Download****, and choose ****Microsoft Excel (xlsx)****. Note that some file resizing may be required in order to download.
