<h1>How to send a personal email in Klaviyo</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to email a single recipient in Klaviyo. To learn how to send a message to a group of contacts all at once, head to [guide to creating and sending an email campaign](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005054847-Guide-to-Creating-and-Sending-an-Email-Campaign).

## Create a personal email

1. Locate a recipient’s profile by searching for their name or email address in the **Search** field at the top of your Klaviyo window.
2. Click ****View messages**** in the top right corner of their profile.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34638517655963)
3. Click ****Send email**** in the top right corner.
   ![Message center, showing the send email button](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34638517662747)
4. Complete the message details (e.g., subject line and sender name/email address).
5. Choose a message type (drag and drop, text only, or HTML).
   - Drag and drop is the most popular choice.
6. Click ****Continue to email****.
7. Choose a template (if required) and add in your custom content.
8. Click ****Exit**** when the message is ready.
9. Click ****Schedule & send email****.
10. Choose a date and time for the message to send (i.e., send now or schedule for a future time), then click ****Schedule email****.

Do not send personal messages to profiles that are suppressed from marketing emails, or to SMS-only profiles. To send a personal SMS in Klaviyo, [use SMS conversations](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059002271-Guide-to-SMS-Conversations-in-Klaviyo).

[Send personal message Video](https://fast.wistia.net/embed/iframe/9sugaegv9n?videoFoam=true)

If you're attempting to re-send a flow or campaign email:

1. [Save the original email as a template](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000102752-Create-Edit-Delete-and-Manage-Templates#save-an-email-as-a-template).
2. Choose ****Drag and drop**** > ****Email: saved****.
3. Click on the template you want.
4. In the modal, select ****Use template****.
   - Please note that if you're using a flow template that contains dynamic event data (e.g., an abandoned cart block), this data can not populate in a personal email.

If you are using the [feature to exclude bot clicks](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004059711#h_01HFEZWHWN7AVGXQB8TGQT6BXG), note that personal email reporting still includes and reflect bot clicks. Learn more about [bot clicks and how they are tracked in Klaviyo](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/22981852783899).

If you have coupons with codes already generated in Klaviyo, you can include these in personal emails as well.

## Cancel a scheduled personal message

You can only cancel a personal message if it hasn’t been sent yet. Personal messages sent using the ****Send Now**** button and messages with a scheduled send time in the past cannot be canceled.

1. From a profile’s ****Messages**** tab, navigate to the ****Scheduled**** tab.
   - If your scheduled message does not appear in the **Scheduled** tab, it has already been sent and cannot be canceled.
2. Hover over the personal message you’d like to cancel in the list of recent messages.
   ![Hovering over a scheduled personal message](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34638502016667)
3. Click ****Delete****.

## Edit a scheduled personal message

To edit a scheduled message that has not yet been sent, first [save the message as a template](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005253868) (if desired), [cancel the message](#h_01G65ZSXNECG0764T6GX2HV1A9), then [create a new personal message](#h_01G65ZT3S7SR0JC0YYDDTZ0RQV).
