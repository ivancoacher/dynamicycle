<h1>How to build an analytics or overview dashboard</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to build a dashboard to visualize all email, SMS, flow, and deliverability trends, and understand the health of your business. Dashboards (also referred to as overview dashboards) provide an all-encompassing, customizable report detailing your success across each marketing channel.

For information on how to interpret the data and performance cards, head to our [Getting started with analytics](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4708299478427) dashboard guide.

## Creating or accessing the dashboard

1. Navigate to ****Analytics > Dashboards****.
2. If this is your first time creating a report, click ****Create Report****. Otherwise, choose a pre-existing report from the list below. You will already have a pre-built dashboard (i.e., **Overview dashboard**) created for you and can customize this dashboard if you like.

![Dashboard view with list of already dashboard below and Create dashboard button in the upper right](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28715964954651)

## Customizing the analytics dashboard

### Choosing a date range

By default, your dashboard will show your performance metrics over the last 30 days.

1. To change the date range, click on the dropdown next to ******Date** **range******.

![Date range dropdown open to show the options for date range choices to select.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/4708268334619/time_range.jpg)

You can adjust the date range to show:

- Week-to-date
- Month-to-date
- Last 7 Days
- Last 30 Days
- Last 90 Days
- Custom (note: you can pull up to 12 months maximum within your date range)

All data views will be based on your account’s local timezone. Dates are inclusive of both start and end date. For example, if you select March 1-March 30, the data is inclusive of both March 1 and March 30.

### Choosing a conversion metric

Besides the date range, you also can choose any account-wide conversion metric. The conversion metrics will pull from any integration or custom conversion-related event and visualize your data.

1. To change the conversion metric, click on the dropdown next to ******Conversion metric******.
2. Choose the conversion metric that suits your current dashboard's needs.

![Conversion metric dropdown with date range options for week, month, and year to date, and Last 7, 30, and 90 days, and custom, and Last 90 days is selected](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/4708431135771/conversion_metric_dropdown.jpg)

### Changing the comparison period

You also have the option to choose whether you want to compare your performance in your date range to a previous period, either the previous year or the previous month.

1. Click on the dropdown next to ******Comparison****** and choose your option (i.e., **Previous Year** or **Previous Period**).

In the example below, the date range is the last 90 days. If you were choose the **Previous Year** option, your data will compare to the same 90days in the previous year. If you choose the **Previous Period**, your data will compare to the same prior date range or, in this case, the 90 days prior to our chosen data rage.

Note that if you use the custom time range, the range cannot exceed 12 months and, if typed in manually, needs to be exactly formatted as shown below.”

![View of the comparison dropdown on the upper right side open showing options to compare data by previous period or year](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/8775263781275/previous.jpg)

For previous year comparisons, we use exact date matches. For example, if your data range is the last 30 days and it’s Sept 1st- Sept 30th of 2022, Klaviyo compares that to Sept 1st- Sept 30th of 2021.

### Refreshing the dashboard

In the upper right-hand corner of the dashboard, the timestamp will appear for the last time a user updated the dashboard.
![The timestamp Of last Updated Feburary 28th next to Add Card button](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/4708722853403/refreshed_date.jpg)

1. To manually refresh the dashboard and the data displayed, click on the ****three dot menu (...)**** in the upper right and select ******Refresh Data******. This will update all the data on the dashboard.

All users on your account will see the same dashboards. The last updated timestamp represents the last time any team member updated the board.

## Adding a card

You can find additional pre-built data cards in the dashboard library. Use these cards to dive deeper into your marketing channel performance and troubleshoot potential issues.

Learn more about the [additional cards available in the library](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4708299478427#using-the-additional-data-library-cards9) and how to use each.

1. Click ****Add Card**** in the upper right-hand corner. A pop-up will appear showing the data view library. This library contains many pre-built cards to serve your data visualization needs.

![Data view library modal with examples of additional cards](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28715964958491)

2. Once you have chosen a pre-built card to add to your dashboard, select it and then click ****Add Data View****.

![Data view library modal with Flow Performance detail card selected](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28715971501851)

The newly added card will appear at the bottom of your dashboard.

There is a limit of 10 cards per dashboard. If you need a different card and have reached the limit, [remove a card](#h_01GTZR10E4NGCYNT56KV1241KY) and then add your new one, or create a new dashboard and add your supplementary cards there. However, if a card is simply appearing empty, head to our [Troubleshooting empty cards on analytics dashboards](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/13466594378523) guide.

## Deleting a card

1. To edit or delete a card, click on the ****three-dot menu (...)**** in the upper right.
2. Select ****Remove data view****.
   ![Three dot menu selected with Remove data view option](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/4714553369883/delete_card.jpg)

## Creating a custom card from scratch

If the pre-built options offered in the data view library do not fit your needs, you have the option to create your own custom cards.

1. To create your own card, click ****Add Card**** and choose the option ****Create from scratch****.

![Example of a blank card with Create from Scratch button in center](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/5977075960731/create_from_scratch.jpg)

2. Choose your metric from the dropdown (e.g., **Checkout Started**).

![Metric dropdown with Checkout Started metric chosen from list](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/5977160984731/create_from_scratch-_metric_dropdown.jpg)

3. Choose how you would like this metric displayed (e.g. **Average, Counts, Unique Counts, or Value**). Note that **Counts** represent the total number of a metric (e.g., total number of opens including duplicate opens by the same subscriber), while **Unique Counts**are the unique number of these (e.g., number of opens per unique subscriber).

![Metric display dropdown with Counts chosen from Unique Counts, Average, and Value options from list](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/5977254373403/create_from_scratch-counts.jpg)

You also have the option to group your data by choosing an option from the Group by dropdown. You can add up to 5 additional filters to narrow down your data (e.g., item or product type).

4. Choose if you would like your data displayed as a table, line graph, bar graph, or as a single number.

5. Once you finish setting up your card, click ****Add Card****.

![Create from scratch custom card modal with options for table, line, graph, or bar graph and conversion metrics, group by, and filtering options](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28715964964123)

If you choose a line or bar graph and a grouping option with no filters, you will see the top 5 values in the graph. To see all **Group by data**, you must choose the table visualization.

#### Editing your custom card

1. If you need to make any adjustments to your custom card, click on the ****3 dot menu (...)**** inside your card.
2. Click ****Edit Card****. This will bring you back to the original card setup in the data view library.

![Three dot menu on right with Edit Card and Remove Data View options](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/5977460578971/create_from_scratch-_edit.png)

## Exporting your complete dashboard

1. Click on the ****3 dot menu (...)**** in the upper right and select ******Print to PDF******. A function will then appear to allow you to print or download the entire dashboard as a PDF.

![View of the Add Card dropdown in the upper right showing the option for Print to PdF second](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/8775427340699/76b898c9-ac9e-48e9-8418-0e7944499094.png)

Cards with graphs will appear without filters applied, and cards with tables will only show the first page of data.

## Adding additional dashboards

By default, you will see a pre-populated dashboard and have the option to create up to 9 additional cards.

1. Navigate to ****Analytics >**** ****Dashboard****.
2. Click ****Create Dashboard**** in the upper right.

![Create dashboard button highlighted in the upper right of the Dashboard section](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28715971506843)Once you create a new dashboard, it will be accessible in your main dashboard section, as shown below.

![Example of new dashboard created in the main dashboard section](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28715964977947)

You can then [add cards](#h_01GTZR6CDM035VX5T8XD6ZKCCX) to your blank dashboard using the data view library, which includes both pre-built cards and a fully configurable **Create your own** option.

### Editing the name of your dashboard

You can edit the name of your dashboard by navigating into your existing dashboard.

1. Click on the ****3 dot menu (...)**** in the upper right.
2. Select ****Edit Name****.

![Three dot menu open with edit name chosen from dropdown](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28715964971931)

## Deleting your dashboard

You can also choose to delete any individual dashboard by either two options.

****Option 1****

1. Navigate to ****Analytics >**** ****Dashboard**** and find the report you wish to delete.
2. Hover over the report in your list and click ****Delete****.
   ![By hovering over a report in the list, the Delet button is shown](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/5977843373851/delete_option_hover.jpg)

****Option 2****

1. Navigate to ****Analytics >**** ****Dashboard****and click directly into the dashboard you wish to delete.
2. Click on the ****3 dot menu (...)**** in the upper right.
3. Select ****Delete Dashboard****.

   ![Three dot menu with Delet dashboard selected from the dropdown](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28715964969755)

Note that if you delete a dashboard, there is no way to retrieve it thereafter. You will need to recreate this dashboard and cards.
