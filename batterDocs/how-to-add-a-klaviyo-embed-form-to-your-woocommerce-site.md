<h1>How to add a Klaviyo embed form to your WooCommerce site</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to add a Klaviyo embed form to your WooCommerce site footer, which requires creating the form in Klaviyo, and then pasting its embed code into your site's files where you want it to appear.

## Before you begin

Before creating an embed form in Klaviyo, you must first [integrate with WooCommerce](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005255808) to ensure that sign-up form functionality is enabled on your site.

## Adding the form’s embed code to your site

First, [create and publish your embed form in Klaviyo](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006897412). This section will go over the next steps of pasting your form’s embed code on your WooCommerce site so it displays and syncs data properly, followed by a video of the process.

1. If you have not already, copy the embed code for your published embed form. You can access the form’s embed code by opening the form in the editor and clicking into the ****Targeting & behavior**** section.
   ![An example form's embed code highlighted to copy from the Display menu within the Targeting and behaviors tab of the form editor.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/32015134440347)
2. Navigate to your WooCommerce dashboard.
3. Select ****Customize your site****.
4. Select ****Widgets****, then choose where you’d like to embed the form.
   - To embed in your site’s footer, choose ****Footer 1****. Collecting subscribers with a footer form is a Klaviyo best practice for list growth.
5. Click ****Add a Widget.****
6. From the menu, select ****Custom**** ****HTML****.
7. In the textbox under **Content**, paste the embed code for your form that you copied from Klaviyo.
8. Select ****Done****.
9. Select ****Publish****.

![2019-07-03_15-35-10.gif](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335751579)

Once you’ve pasted the embed code and saved the changes in WooCommerce, navigate back to your website and refresh the page. As long as the form is published in Klaviyo, site visitors will see it live on your site and have the option to join your list by completing it.

If you don’t see your form, see [troubleshooting embed forms](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006897412#h_01JFAQY2NHMG3PPV523K4ESXEV).
