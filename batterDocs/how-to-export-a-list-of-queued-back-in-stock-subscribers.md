<h1>How to export a list of queued back in stock subscribers</h1>

## You will learn

Learn how to export your currently queued back in stock subscribers to a CSV file. In Klaviyo, you can export a list of profiles currently waiting for a certain product to come back in stock and how long they've been waiting. This list can teach you more about your audience and in-demand products and can help inform you about what types of products to offer, who to target in future campaigns, and more.

Note that only [owners and admins](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005231648) can export the list of queued back in stock subscribers.

## Exporting your queued back in stock subscribers

1. Navigate to the ****Flows**** tab.

2. Find your back in stock flow and then click on the back in stock delay component.

3. In the sidebar, click ****View back in stock reports****.

![view back in stock reports button](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/33677058667931)

4. In the upper right, click the ****Export****dropdown.

![Inside back in stock reports in back in stock activity, with Export menu expanded above](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717382963867)

5. In the dropdown, click ****Currently Queued Subscribers CSV****.

![Export currently queued subscribers](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/33677027728027)

After you select this, you will be automatically redirected to the ****Recent Downloads**** page. Here, the export will show as **Processing** until it's ready to be downloaded.

6. Click on your applicable file to download. The **Currently Queued Subscribers CSV** will include the following columns:

- **Email**
- **Date added**
- **SKU**
- **Variant ID**
- **Product title**
- **Variant title**
- **Unit price**
