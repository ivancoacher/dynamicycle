<h1>How to add or delete users and manage permissions</h1>

Only Owners and Admins can add or remove users from an account.

Learn how to add new users to a Klaviyo account and set their roles.

This article is specific to adding or removing users who are not the Owner in a regular Klaviyo account. If this is not what you're looking for, you might want our articles on [transferring account ownership](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051946112) or [adding or removing users in a portfolio](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/25185184021019).

## Add users

1. Click your account name in the bottom left corner.
   ![Opening the account dropdown to access your settings](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40690528471579)
2. Navigate to ****Settings > Users****.
3. Click ****Add new user****.
   ![Users tab showing the Add new user button](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40690528474523)
4. Type in the email address of the new user.
   ![Modal to add the email address, language, and role for the new user](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40690574286363)
5. Under **Language**, click the dropdown to select a preferred language for the new user's account. Note that at this time only English US and Français are supported. The email invitation sent to this user will be in the language you choose here. [Learn more about organization and personal language settings for your account](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/25956849989531).
6. Under **Role**, select 1 of the following:
   - Admin
   - Manager
   - Analyst
   - Support
   - Campaign Coordinator
   - Content Creator
7. Click ****Add User****.

Once you add your new user, they will receive an email from our Support team with a link to set up their account. They will experience Klaviyo in the language you set, but they can [change their personal language preferences if needed](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/25956849989531).

### Accept or decline your account access link

1. Navigate to your inbox and search for an email from our Support team with the subject **New account invitation**.

   Before accepting the invitation to join the account, be sure to log out of any other Klaviyo accounts to ensure that the button in the email functions properly.

   ![Email to activate a Klaviyo account](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713338097947)
2. If you accept the access you will be added as a user to the account. If you decline, you will not be added to the account.

### Resend a new user invitation

1. Click your account name in the bottom left corner.
2. Navigate to ****Settings > Users****.
3. Select the ****Inactive users****tab.
   ![Inactive users tab in the Users page](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40690528485403)
4. Click the ****3 dots**** next to the user that you want to resend the invitation to.
5. Select ****Resend Invites****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40690528487067)
6. Check the box next to the email addresses which you'd like to resend the account invitation to.
7. Click ****Send****. You'll see a green banner informing you that your invite has resent.

## Remove or delete users

You cannot delete the Owner of an account. To remove an Owner, you must first [transfer account ownership](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051946112) to someone else.

1. Navigate to ****Settings > Users****.
2. Click the 3 vertical dots next to the user you want to remove.
3. Select ****Delete user****.
   ![Dropdown for active users, showing the delete button](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40690528490651)
4. In the modal that appears, click ****Delete user****.

## Manage permissions

On the settings page for your account, you can also:

- Change the role of existing users by clicking on their email address and updating their settings.
- See which users have set up their accounts and which still have their email invitation pending.
- Resend invitation emails to users who have not completed setting up their accounts.
- Click on a user to view their details, which include:
  - Name
  - Email
  - When they were added
  - When they last logged in
  - Their current role
