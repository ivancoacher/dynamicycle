## Overview

Our [Magento 2 integration](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005254348#sync-a-magento-signup-form-to-a-klaviyo-list) documents cover syncing a Magento sign-up form to a Klaviyo list for any future subscribers. This article covers uploading any existing subscribers to your Klaviyo list.

You can also sync customers at checkout for both email and [SMS](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058698511) with Magento 2; however, that is not covered here.

## Uploading existing newsletter subscribers for Magento 2

From your Magento store admin click on ****Customers > All Customers****.
![mage1_newsletterUsers.png](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723506096923)

Filter your customers by **Newsletter Subscriber** and export the list as a CSV file.

From your Klaviyo account, [edit and upload your CSV file](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005078487-Migrate-Existing-Subscribers-and-Unsubscribes-into-Klaviyo#import-contacts-from-a-csv-file) to the Klaviyo list you want to sync your Magento sign-up form to.
