Learn how to sync reviews with Google Shopping so shoppers from across the web can see your customer ratings. This process can take upwards of 2 weeks to complete, so start the process early. However, if you've previously synced reviews to Google Shopping through another provider, they will continue to display in search results throughout the migration process.

Reviews must have text to appear in the review feed. Reviews with only a star rating will be excluded. Only reviews for published products will be pushed to Google Shopping.

## Google’s content requirements for reviews

Google’s review requirements are strict. If your review feed does not meet these criteria, your feed may be removed from Google Shopping and reviews you’ve submitted won’t appear there.

In brief, these requirements are:

- Your feed must contain at least 50 reviews.
- You must sync all your product reviews to Google at least once per month. Klaviyo recommends a daily sync.
  - “All reviews” means you cannot filter reviews based on star rating. You must share both low- and high-rating reviews. The reviews feed Klaviyo creates for you contains all reviews except those that are rejected.
- Reviews must meet Google’s content guidelines. They cannot include spam, identifying information (phone numbers, emails, URLs, full names, and confidential information like credit card numbers), obscenity, offensive language, reviews written by employees or friends, reviews that were paid for, illegal content, copyrighted content, plagiarized reviews, sexually explicit material, hate speech, cross-promotion of other products, off-topic reviews, impersonation of another entity, reviews that have been translated from the language they were originally written in, and duplicate content. Learn more about [Google’s content policies for reviews](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6098512#zippy=%2Creview-feeds-must-adhere-to-content-policies).
- Your feed should include relevant, high-quality reviews. Reviews that consist only of emojis or messages like “love it!” aren’t helpful to a potential customer and may not be displayed.
- You should only collect and share reviews that you own. Reviews collected through Klaviyo reviews meet this criteria.
- If you include customer images in your feed, they should be high-quality, taken by your customers (not you or a third party), and minimally filtered.

This is an overview of Google’s requirements. [Read Google’s requirements in full.](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6098512)

## Use unique product identifiers

When you submit your reviews to Google, those reviews are matched to the relevant product using a global unique product identifier like a global trade item number (GTIN). If a GTIN is not available, Google can match a review to a product using a SKU, a brand and manufacturer part number (MPN) pair, or a product URL.

## Create a Google Merchant Center account

Visit [Google Merchant Center](https://merchants.google.com/signup) and create an account.

## Upload your products and wait for approval

Learn how to create a product feed using [Google’s documentation](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7439058). Alternatively, use a [Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/search?q=google%20shopping%20feed) or [WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com/product-category/woocommerce-extensions/marketing-extensions/advertising-and-promotions/?q=google+product+feed&collections=product&page=1) app to generate your feed. Once you submit your feed to Google, it can take up to a few weeks for it to be approved. In the meantime, proceed with the steps below.

## Sign up for product ratings

To sign up for Google Merchants Product Ratings, fill out their interest form.

1. Navigate to the [Product Ratings Interest Form](https://support.google.com/merchants/troubleshooter/10994881).
2. Under **Do you work with an approved third-party reviews aggregator?**, choose ****No****. This simply means you’ll upload your reviews through a manual process for now. Klaviyo reviews is working to become an approved third-party reviews aggregator soon.
3. Complete other questions based on your business.
4. Once you submit the form, Google will reach out to you within a few days with the following message:
   **Your aggregator, 'Klaviyo reviews', isn’t part of the Product Ratings program yet, so they won’t be able to submit a direct feed for you. Instead, you’ll need to send a direct product ratings feed to Google Merchant Center.**
   **Please let us know if you would like to send a direct Product Ratings feed by replying to this email.**
5. Reply to this email confirming you’ll submit a direct product ratings feed.
6. Once Google confirms, you’ll see the ****Product reviews**** tab in your Google Merchant Center account under **Marketing**.

## Generate a reviews feed

1. Click ****Reviews**** in the left-side navigation in Klaviyo.
2. Click ****Settings****.
3. Click ****Google Shopping Syndication****.
4. Toggle **Enable Google Shopping Feed** on.
5. Optional: Toggle **Use SKU as MPN** on to use your product SKU as the manufacturer part number. The default is off; turn this setting on if you don't have UPCs set up for your products in your ecommerce platform.
6. Click ****Update Google Shopping Settings****.
7. Copy the URL that appears under **Your Google Shopping Feed URL:**; you’ll need it in the next section.

## Upload your reviews feed

Klaviyo’s support team is unable to troubleshoot problems with Google Merchant Center or Google Shopping. If you encounter any problems, reach out to [Google’s support](https://support.google.com/merchants/?hl=en#topic=7259123).

1. Navigate to Google Merchant Center.
2. Click ****Marketing**** from the menu on the left.
3. Select ****Product reviews****.
4. Click ****Product reviews feeds**** in the top right corner.
5. Click ****+**** to add a feed.
6. Choose a name for the feed and choose ****Scheduled fetch****.
7. Click ****Continue****.
8. Add the URL you copied from the last section.
9. For **Fetch frequency**, choose ****Daily****.
10. Leave the **Username** and **Password** fields blank.
11. Click ****Create feed****.

It can take up to a month for Google to review the feed, and new reviews will not be visible in Google Shopping until they approve it. Respond to any emails from Google in a timely manner to expedite the process.

### What happens to my existing reviews on Google Shopping?

Your existing product reviews will continue to appear in Google Shopping search results if you synced them from your previous reviews provider. You will not experience any downtime or disruption with Google Shopping while you migrate to Klaviyo.

## Outcome

Once you’ve completed these steps, your product reviews will appear automatically in Google Shopping. Seeing these reviews across platforms can lend validity to your brand’s image and encourage traffic to your store from browsers who otherwise might not have found you.
