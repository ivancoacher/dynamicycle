## You will learn

Learn how to locate the unique ID assigned to every list in Klaviyo. In addition to lists, every user, flow, campaign, segment, and more.

## Find your list ID

To find the list ID for an existing Klaviyo list:

1. Navigate to the ****Lists & Segments****tab of your account.
2. Click on a particular list.
3. Click into the ****Settings**** tab.
4. Here, you will find your list ID near the top of the page.
   ![find a list ID.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28704484548635)

Additionally, you can find a list's ID in the URL in your browser when viewing this list. In the URL, you will see a short series of numbers and letters — this is the list ID.

![A list ID found in a list's URL](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28704484547739)
