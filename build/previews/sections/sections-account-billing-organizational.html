• How to adjust the time zone setting for your account

    Your account's time zone is used to display dates and times for all tracked events. If it's set incorrectly, it may affect the accuracy of tracked email and event activity, as well as your overall reporting in Klaviyo. This article explains how to adjust your time zone to accurately reflect your location.

  • How to change the currency for your account

    Learn how to change the default currency symbol in your Klaviyo account from the dollar sign ($) to another currency. This change is useful if you conduct business in a different currency, as it can help save you time and minimize the risk of using an incorrect symbol.

  • How to change the language for your account

    Learn how to change the organization and personal language settings for your Klaviyo account. Changing the language also updates the regional formatting (e.g., for numbers and dates).

  • How to change your account name

    Learn how to change your account name (also called your organization name) in Klaviyo. Knowing how to do this is helpful if your company is rebranding, there’s a mistake in the current name, or you simply want to edit it in some way.

  • How to change your account's organization name

    Learn how to change your account's organization name (also called your "company name") in Klaviyo. Knowing how to do this is helpful if your company is rebranding, there’s a mistake in the current name, or you simply want to edit it in some way.

  • How to rebrand in Klaviyo

    Learn how to change your domain name during a rebrand and best practices for maintaining good deliverability throughout the change.

  • How to update your billing address or billing email address

    Only account Owners and Admins can update the billing address or billing email address.

  • How to update your credit card information

    Learn how to update the credit card information associated with your account. Note that Klaviyo does not support multiple credit cards in a single account.

  • How to update your industry and vertical in Klaviyo

    Learn how to view and edit your business' industry and vertical in Klaviyo. Your Klaviyo account should always accurately reflect your business’ industry and vertical.

  • Understanding the activity logger (beta)

    The activity logger is a self-service audit trail that provides visibility into all actions performed within your Klaviyo account. Instead of contacting Klaviyo support to investigate account changes, you can now independently track who made a change, what that change was, and when it happened.
