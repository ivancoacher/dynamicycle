    Learn how to set up a tap-to-text sign-up form to collect phone numbers from new prospects. With tap-to-text, site visitors can click a button in your sign-up form to automatically open their text messaging app where Klaviyo has added a message. They only need to send the message to subscribe.

  • How to create an SMS form that targets email subscribers

    Learn how to create a sign-up form that retargets your existing email subscribers to ask them if they want to sign up for SMS marketing. Collecting phone numbers from your email subscribers enables you to engage across multiple channels, and tailor messages based on subscriber preferences to create more personalized experiences.
