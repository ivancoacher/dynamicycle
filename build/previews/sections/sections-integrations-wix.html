• Getting started with Wix

    Learn how to easily integrate Klaviyo with your Wix site in order to sync customer profile, order, and catalog information to Klaviyo. With this data, you’ll be able to reach customers with targeted messaging through segmentation, automated flows, and campaigns.

  • How to use Wix contact labels to segment customers

    Learn how to use Wix contact labels to segment customers in Klaviyo. Wix contact labels are assigned as a list type custom property on each customer’s Klaviyo profile. These profiles sync into Klaviyo through your Wix integration.

    Learn what data is synced from Wix to Klaviyo, how to view it, and what properties Wix events contain. Additionally, learn how to view your Wix data in Klaviyo.
