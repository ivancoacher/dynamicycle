  • How to edit the location of your tracking pixel

    Klaviyo tracks when a customer opens or clicks an email using a clear pixel. By default, the pixel is located at the bottom of your emails but you can adjust its placement. If you send very large messages, then choosing to have this pixel at the top of your emails instead of the bottom can improve open tracking that is otherwise cut or clipped by some inbox

  • Understanding message conversion tracking

    Learn how conversion tracking works for email, SMS, and mobile push messaging and how to edit these settings. For each campaign and flow sent, Klaviyo automatically tracks conversions. This allows you to analyze the performance of your marketing channels and their individual success.
