• How to add a tag to a campaign or flow

    In Klaviyo, tags act as labels that help you organize and manage your account’s content efficiently. Tags can be attached to various elements, such as campaigns and flows, allowing you to quickly identify, categorize, sort, and filter data.

  • How to add a tag to a list or segment

    For lists and segments, you can either add tags when creating a list or segment, or you can add tags retroactively when they are already built in your account. This article will cover both methods.
