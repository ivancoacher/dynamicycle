• Frequently asked questions about GDPR

    GDPR stands for the General Data Protection Regulation which is a law enacted by the European Commission in 2016 that went into effect on May 25, 2018. It’s designed to protect the privacy of all EU citizens, including when those citizens engage with businesses located outside the European Union, by imposing regulations around personal data.
