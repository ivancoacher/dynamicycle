了解如何安排 Klaviyo 注册表单在您的网站上显示或隐藏的时间，以便您可以控制购物者何时与它们互动。 Scheduling your sign-up forms helps save time and energy that would otherwise be spent manually setting a form live. Schedules are also helpful to use when building promotional holiday sign-up forms, or running limited time sales or special events. Note that if you enable A/B testing on a live form, any schedule associated with that form will be disregarded while the A/B test is running. ## 开始之前

在[**注册表单**](https://www.klaviyo.com/forms) [选项卡](https://www.klaviyo.com/forms) 中，您可以查看您在 Klaviyo 中创建的所有注册表单的列表，包括已在您的网站上发布并生效的注册表单以及仍处于草稿模式的注册表单。如果您是 Klaviyo 新手并且还没有任何注册表单，请[创建新表单](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026474752-Getting-started-with-signup-forms) 以查看此表单列表。如果您计划安排表单在特定时间发布，请确保在表单上线之前完成[自定义表单](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026474752-Getting-started-with-signup-forms#design-your-form7)，以便确保其在网站上的外观和行为符合您的要求。 ![scheddd.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720772995611)

在表单列表视图中，“状态”列将反映您的表单是否为**实时**、**编辑**（实时且包含未发布的编辑内容）、**草稿**或**已计划**。 Scheduled forms will also show a description of the schedule plans beneath the status icon. ## 安排表格

从列表视图中，您可以安排草稿表单何时向访问者发布，也可以安排实时表单何时停止显示并恢复为草稿。 ### 安排草稿表格上线

1. 导航至****注册表单****选项卡。 2. Next to the form that you would like to schedule, click the 3dots menu. Draft forms will have a gray status icon next to them indicating that they are not currently published on your website. 3. From the dropdown of options, click ****Schedule****. ![scheddd1.5.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720761242395)
4. Select a start date and time for when you want shoppers to start seeing this form on your site. ![scheduledforms2..jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720761215003)
5. 设置您所需的时区。 ![scheduledform3.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720772979483)

   时区默认为您自己的时区； however, you can change this to any other timezone. Click on the dropdown to select a different location, or type one into the **Filter** box to automatically set that location’s local timezone. 6. Next, you have the option to set an end date for when you want to stop showing shoppers this form. If you schedule the form without setting an end date, your form will remain live on your website until you manually toggle it back to draft. ![scheduleform4real.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720772982299)
7. Click ****Schedule**** to save this schedule plan. ### 安排何时暂停实时表单

1. 导航至****注册表单****选项卡。 2. Next to the form that you would like to pause or stop displaying based on your schedule, click the 3dots menu. Note that live forms will have a green status icon next to them indicating that they are not currently published on your website. 3. 单击****计划****。 ![scheddd1.5.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720761242395)
4. Select a date and time for when you want to stop showing this form to shoppers on your site. ![scheduleform6.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720772984859)
5. 设置您的时区。 ![scheduledform7.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720772990747)

   The timezone defaults to your own, however you can change this to any other timezone. Click on the dropdown to select a different location, or type one into the **Filter** box to automatically set that location’s local timezone. 6. Next, you have the option to select a date and time for when you want the form to go live again. If you schedule the form without setting an end date, it will remain in draft mode after it’s automatically taken down. 7. Click ****Schedule**** to save this schedule plan. ## 编辑或删除表单的时间表

在表单列表视图中，表单旁边将出现一个蓝色的 **已计划** 图标，表明它已计划，并附有所设置计划的说明。您可以继续更改为表单设置的计划：

1. 单击要编辑的计划表单旁边的 3 点菜单。 2. 从下拉列表中，单击****编辑计划****。 ![scheddd3.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720761245595)
3. 更改开始日期和结束日期。 4. 单击****重新安排****。 ![scheduledform9.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720772993179)
5. 要完全删除表单的时间表，请单击****删除时间表****。这不会删除表单本身，但会删除您为设置表单或草稿表单而创建的任何计划。如果您在计划时间之前手动将表单设置为实时或草稿，则表单将自动跳过第一个计划步骤。例如，如果您计划将草稿表单在 11 月 14 日上线，但随后手动将其设置为在 11 月 13 日上线，则将跳过计划的步骤，因为已采取操作并且表单已上线。如果您计划了后续操作（例如结束日期），则该操作仍会自动发生，除非您删除该计划。
