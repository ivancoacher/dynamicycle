You must be an Owner, Admin, or Manager to change the quiet hour settings for an account.

## You will learn

Learn how to change your quiet hour settings in Klaviyo, including:

- Changing whether quiet hours are based on either the phone number's:
  - Area code (for the US and Canada)
    Or
  - Eastern Time zone
- Updating when quiet hours are in effect for flow messages

For more information about quiet hours, check out our [article on understanding quiet hours](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408737146651).

## Before you begin

Please note the following:

- Several countries have laws against sending during quiet hours.
- Quiet hours are only available for flows; however, you will get a warning if you attempt to send a campaign during quiet hours.
- Klaviyo does not set quiet hours on a daily basis. Your chosen quiet hours will apply to all days of the week.
- It’s recommended that you set up quiet hours so that flow messages are not sent between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. in your recipient’s local time (certain states may have stricter quiet hour rules).
  - If you send to recipients in Alaska or Hawaii, either use quiet hours by area code or adjust the hours so that you aren’t sending between 8 p.m. and 2 p.m. ET.

## Change whether quiet hours is based on area code or eastern time zone

1. Click your account name in the bottom left corner.
2. Navigate to ****Settings > SMS > Sender preferences****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/36748683955355)
3. Scroll to the **SMS quiet hours** section.
4. Check the option you want to use for quiet hours:
   - Use phone number area code (recommended).
   - Use eastern time zone.
     ![Screenshot 2025-09-23 at 2.51.27 PM.png](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/41370514460187)
5. Click ****Save**** to confirm your changes.

## Update quiet hours window

To adjust your account’s default Klaviyo SMS quiet hours:

1. Navigate to your account name in the lower left-hand corner.
2. Click ****Settings > SMS > Sender preferences****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/36748683960219)
3. Review the times listed for quiet hours.
   The 2 times will represent the range that a message cannot be sent in the time zone being used. For example, you could adjust your quiet hours so that a message cannot be sent between 7:30 p.m. and 11:30 a.m. Eastern Time in the United States and Canada.
4. Click the dropdown under **Stop sending** to change when quiet hours should start (i.e., what time the flow stops sending SMS).
   ![Screenshot 2025-09-23 at 2.53.07 PM.png](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/41370503827099)
5. Click the dropdown under **Resume sending** to change when quiet hours should end (i.e., what time the flow can begin to send out SMS).
6. Once you update your quiet hours, click ****Save**** at the top of the page to save your setup.
