You must have access to your Meta Business Manager and WhatsApp Business Account (WABA).

Set up and update your WhatsApp display name so customers see your business name instead of your phone number.

Following Meta’s guidelines helps you get approved faster and avoid rejections.

Meta reviews and approves display names.

## Edit your display name in Klaviyo

To change your display name in Klaviyo:

1. Select your account name in the lower left.
2. Navigate to ****Settings > WhatsApp****.
3. Find the **WhatsApp phone numbers** section.
4. Click the 3-dot menu next to a phone number.
5. Click ****View****.
   ![Phone number with the 3-dot menu opened](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/6ct6b26e/help-center-prod/22fc2411707732cfcdffb60de30adaca9c4f5b9e-1284x232.png)
6. In the right-panel, find the **Display name** field.
7. Edit the display name, then click ****Save****.

Any edit to a display name must be reviewed by Meta, which takes a few business days. The change does not show to customers until Meta approves.

****Change display name in Meta****

Follow these steps to update your display name.

1. Open Meta Business Manager and select your business.
2. Navigate to ****WhatsApp Manager****.
3. Go to ****Account tools**** > ****Phone numbers > Profile****.
4. Enter your new display name.
5. Submit your name for review.

Meta typically reviews display names within a few days.

## Display name guidelines

Follow Meta’s rules to improve approval success. For full details, please see [Meta's support documentation](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/757569725593362).

### What your display name must include

Your display name must:

- Represent your business, product, service, or department
- Match your external branding
- Clearly relate to your legal business name
- Contain at least 3 characters
- Follow [WhatsApp Commerce and Business policies](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy)

### What your display name cannot include

Your display name must not:

- Use all caps unless it matches your branding
- Include emojis or special symbols
- Contain unnecessary extra words
- Be formatted as a URL
- Include “Official” or “Verified”
- Reference Meta product names
- Be a generic term or location alone

## What happens after you submit

After submission, Meta reviews your display name and assigns a status:

- Approved
- Rejected
- Under review

  Your display name becomes visible when:
- Your Meta Business Portfolio is verified, or
- You reach at least ****2,000 daily business-initiated conversations****

## Troubleshooting

### My display name was rejected

Common reasons:

- Does not match website branding
- Language mismatch
- Formatting issues

To fix this:

1. Match your display name to your website.
2. Update your site if needed.
3. Resubmit in Meta.

### My display name is approved, but customers still see my phone number

Your account has not met visibility requirements.

You need:

- A verified Meta Business Portfolio, or
- At least ****2,000 daily business-initiated conversations****

### I want the blue checkmark (Official Business Account)

Your display name must be approved first.

Fix any issues, then apply in WhatsApp Manager.
