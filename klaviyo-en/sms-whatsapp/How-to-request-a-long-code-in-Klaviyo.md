To request a long code, you must:

- Be an Owner or Admin.
- Have a paid SMS plan.

## You will learn

Find out how to request or register a long code so that you can start sending SMS.

Long codes are available for the following countries:

- Australia
- Austria
- Belgium
- Denmark
- Finland
- Germany
- Hungary
- Ireland
- Italy
- Norway
- Poland
- Portugal
- Spain
- Sweden
- Switzerland
- The Netherlands
- United Kingdom

Not sure which number type is best for you? Check out this article that [compares the different SMS number types and their advantages](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/6637671573403).

## Before you begin

Some countries have requirements when applying for a long code. Click on the subsection below that applies to your country to view the requirements.

### Australia

Australia requires that every business requesting a long code provides certain information. You’ll need to show proof of your business’s:

- Name
- ID number (i.e., Australian business number)

A commercial registry (or equivalent documentation) can be used as proof of all of the above.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ****Required information**** | ****Accepted documents**** |
| Business name | Commercial registry (or equivalent documentation) |
| Business address | - Commercial registry or equivalent showing address - Utility bill - Tax notice - Rent receipt - Title deed |
| Australian business number | Commercial registry (or equivalent documentation) |

###

### Belgium

To register a Belgian long code (LC), ****the business must have a registered business address in Belgium****. Only businesses with a Belgian address are eligible to obtain a Belgian LC.

Belgium requires the following information to register a long code:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ****Required information**** | ****Supporting documents**** |
| Business name | Commercial registry (or equivalent documentation) |
| Business address | - Commercial registry or equivalent showing address - Utility bill - Tax notice - Rent receipt - Title deed |
| Business registration number | Commercial registry (or equivalent documentation). |
| Personal email address for a point of contact | Provide an email address that meets the following criteria:   - Use an individual's personal contact email and not a generic support, help, or customer service address. - Avoid Google, Yahoo, or any other non-business-related email address where possible. - Use a business domain and ensure the domain aligns with the company name information being submitted (e.g., if the domain differs from the company name, this likely to be rejected). |

###

### Germany

To register a German long code (LC), ****the business must have a registered business address in Germany****. Only businesses with a German address are eligible to obtain a German LC.

Germany requires the following information to register a long code:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ****Required information**** | ****Supporting documents**** |
| Point of contact | - First name - Last Name - Email address |
| Business name | Commercial registry (or equivalent) |
| Business registration number | Commercial registry (or equivalent) |
| Business website | Valid website address |
| Business address | Commercial registry (or equivalent) |

###

### Ireland

Ireland requires that every business requesting a long code provides certain information. You’ll need to show proof of your business’s:

- Name
- Business registration

For the documentation, you can submit proof of business registration from an authorized government authority. Examples include:

- IE Company Registration Office proof of business registration
- UK Companies House certificate of incorporation

###

### Italy

To obtain a long code in Italy, every business must provide:

- Business address
- Proof of business address

| --- | --- |
| ****Required information**** | ****Accepted documents**** |
| Business address | Any address |
| Proof of address | - Utility bill - Tax notice - Rent receipt - Title deed |

## Best practices

Before requesting a long code, let your SMS recipients know that you’ll be changing your number. For details, check out this article on how to [alert your subscribers about a change in number](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403980438555).

Long code requests have different approval times depending on the country. In most cases, the long codes are available immediately or, in Australia, Belgium, and Ireland, in 1-3 business days.

Once your request has been approved, your long code will be automatically assigned to your account. For most countries, your long code will be the default sending number for all messages for the corresponding country.

## Request a long code in Klaviyo

If you send to multiple countries, each country must have a separate long code, so you must repeat this process per country.

1. Navigate to ****Settings**** > ****SMS****.
2. Go to the **Sending** **information** section.
   ![Sender information section showing countries and their corresponding phone numbers](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/32597363173787)
3. Either:
   - Select ****Add country**** (if you don't see your country listed), then seach for that country in the resulting modal.
   - In the line with the flag for your country, click the 3 vertical dots on the right-hand side and click ****Edit****.
4. In the modal, choose ****Long**** ****code****.
   - This is the only option for Belgium.
     ![Edit sending number for a country](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/32597363177371)
5. Click ****Next****.
6. If required for your country:
   - Add information about your business. Information entered here must align with your business registration documents.
   - Upload your supporting documents as proof of business registration.
   - Select ****Submit**** ****Request****.
7. Click ****Exit****.

## Outcome

If your long code request requires documentation, it will be available in 1 to 3 business days. Otherwise, it will be available immediately. Once available, you can start adding it to your sign-up forms, website, or email banners.
