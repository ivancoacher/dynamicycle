## You will learn

Learn how to create and share a link to collect SMS consent through social media campaigns or emails. After you post this link, anyone who clicks on it can easily sign up for SMS. Those on mobile devices can opt in with tap-to-text, while those on desktop are directed to a subscribe page.

## Before you begin

Before generating an SMS opt-in link, you must have already turned on SMS in your Klaviyo account.

Also, keep the following in mind:

****Subscribe links only work for 1 sending number****

For instance, if you use a toll-free number for both the US and Canada, you can use a single subscribe link to collect SMS subscribers in both countries.

However, say you use a short code in Canada. In that case, you need to create a separate subscribe link for each country. You then need to display one link only to the US and the other only to Canada.

****The mobile experience doesn’t work for branded sender IDs****

If you use a branded sender ID, you can only collect consent via the desktop experience (i.e., on a subscribe page).

Further, if you only have a branded sender ID in your Klaviyo account (i.e., no number that can receive messages), you must get this link by [creating a regular subscribe page](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005251988).

****Include disclosure language anywhere you post this link****

A big part of SMS compliance is making sure people know what they’re opting into anywhere you collect consent. Whenever you use an SMS subscribe link, you need to add [disclosure language](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412878737051) along with it.

## Generate a subscribe link

Do not copy the link when editing the subscribe page. Only copy the link from either the **Growth tools** or **Short link**page or in the actual **Manage subscribe link** page.

There are 2 ways you can generate the SMS subscribe link: either from your SMS settings or from the **Growth tools** page.

1. To create an SMS subscribe link, either:

   - Navigate to ****Audience > Growth tools**** in the left-hand side.
   - Navigate to ****Settings > SMS > Short link****.
2. Next to **Create your first subscribe link** , click ****Create****.
3. Under **Mobile experience**, click the dropdown to choose the SMS keyword you want to use.
   Note that the keyword determines which list subscribers are added to for both the mobile and desktop experiences.
4. Choose the country (or countries) for this subscribe link.
   ![Section to pick your keyword and countries for your subscribe link's mobile experience](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716355853467)
5. Add in the message that will populate in the subscriber’s messaging app.
6. To view or change the subscribe page, click ****Edit subscribe page****.

   - If you get the option to choose between a default page or customizing the page for this list, select ****Customize for this List****. This will create a new set of consent pages (i.e., preference page, subscribe page, unsubscribe page) specifically for subscribers joining this list.
7. In some cases, Klaviyo will automatically adjust the subscribe page to collect phone number consent; however, you may need to do so manually:

   - Drag in a phone number field.
   - Do not delete the disclosure language, and place it above the button.
   - Hover over the email field then click the trashcan icon to delete it.
     - Collecting email and SMS consent separately is best practice for list growth and compliance with wireless carriers.![Example of a subscribe page created for SMS](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716355849371)

   If you're an alcohol or firearms-related brand, you may need to add a step with an [age-gating field](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17252552814875) to your subscribe page.
8. Check your disclosure language to make sure it:

   - Mentions your company or SMS program name.
   - Links to both your terms of service and privacy policy.![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/30081393739803)
9. Click ****Confirmation page**** above the preview of your subscribe page.
   ![Highlighted step for confirmation page above the preview for the subscribe page](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716332921755)
10. Click ****Custom redirect**** in the left sidebar.
11. Check the **Skip page and redirect** box.
12. In the **Redirect URL** field, input the page you want to send subscribers (home page, featured collection, etc.)
13. Click ****Publish**** in the upper right to save your edits to the subscribe page, then click ****Exit****.
14. Navigate to either:

    - ****Audience > Growth tools > Create your first subscribe link****.
    - ****Settings > SMS > Short links****
15. Name the link something descriptive.
    ![Create subscribe link page showing an example subscribe link named FB link.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/30080347510427)
16. Click ****Save**** to save this link.
17. Click ****Copy link**** to use the subscribe link.
18. Copy the SMS disclosure language and include it wherever you post this link.

## Outcome

Once you’re done, you can post this link anywhere you want to collect SMS consent, whether that’s in a targeted ad, post on Instagram, or elsewhere.

You can even turn this link into a QR code that you can include it in your customers’ packages or on a sign at your store.
