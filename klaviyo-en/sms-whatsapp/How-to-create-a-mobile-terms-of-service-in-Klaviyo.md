## You will learn

Learn how to automatically generate your own mobile terms of service (TOS) in Klaviyo. In addition to generating a mobile TOS that you can customize to fit your needs, Klaviyo can also host the TOS for you, and you can add a link to your TOS anywhere (like forms where you collect SMS consent). Alternatively, you can copy the text in the TOS and paste into another page, like your existing TOS page.

### Why do I need a mobile TOS?

Before you begin collecting SMS consent from your customers, it’s important to update your terms of service so that subscribers know what to expect. Many countries require a TOS for any SMS program, and Klaviyo makes creating one simple.

## Best practice for using Klaviyo's mobile TOS

Your mobile TOS doesn’t need to be part of your normal TOS; it can be separate. What’s important is that you link to your mobile TOS anywhere you collect SMS consent.

If you're using Klaviyo's TOS as a base, note that you will need to update the language in for the following situations:

- If you are ****NOT**** using SMS for abandoned cart messages, then remove “(e.g., cart reminders)” from your terms of service.
- If you are either:
  - Exclusively using a branded sender ID: remove any language about texting in keywords (e.g., STOP or HELP), since this number type can't receive these keywords.
  - Never using a branded sender ID: remove the language about clicking the unsubscribe link in any text message to cancel).
- If you are not planning to use SMS for either service-related or promotional messages, remove any language from the TOS (second paragraph in the example).
  - Similarly, if you are not planning to use SMS for a specific type of message (e.g., account alerts), remove mention of those messages.

## Create a mobile TOS in Klaviyo

Follow the instructions below to learn how to find the mobile TOS generated by Klaviyo:

1. Click on your brand name in the bottom left corner.
2. Navigate to ****Settings > SMS****.
3. Select the ****Terms of Service**** tab.
4. Under **Terms of Service**, click ****Create mobile terms of service****.
   ![The Mobile Terms of Service creation menu on the Terms of Service settings page.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/30080340040091)
5. Review and (if necessary) update the following information in the modal:
   - The full legal name of your company
   - The email address for your Support team
   - The types of promotional SMS messages you plan to send
   - The types of transactional SMS messages you plan to send
   - A link to your privacy policy

- If you have not yet done so, work with your legal/compliance advisors to update your privacy policy to cover your SMS program.
  - If you have already updated your privacy policy to include information on your SMS program, check the box that says ****I have updated my privacy policy to include SMS sending information****.
    ![Mobile Terms of Service creation form with fields for company name, support email, message types, and privacy policy URL.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/30080340041243)

1. Click ****Continue****.
2. On the following page, review the generated mobile TOS, and update or customize it to make sure it fits your company's compliance requirements as needed. We recommend consulting your legal/compliance advisors and having them review the TOS.
3. Choose 1 of the following options:
   - [Have Klaviyo host the mobile TOS](#h_01GJDD7RVWM9CHDFV7DNE83PPJ) (the default).
   - [Add to an existing TOS](#h_01GJDD808M418VAXHEX2RSG7FE).
4. Regardless of which option you choose, check the box that states: ****I acknowledge that these generated Mobile Terms of Service do not constitute legal advice. I agree to consult with legal counsel to make sure that I am complying with applicable laws in connection with my SMS marketing activities.****

### Have Klaviyo host the mobile TOS

This option is best for those who want to direct customers specifically to a mobile TOS, as opposed to adding it to your existing TOS.

If you choose to allow Klaviyo to host the mobile TOS, we will supply a link you can use in your site navigation, checkout page, forms, emails, etc.

To get this link:

1. Click ****Save**** after you check the acknowledgement box in step 9 of the last section.
2. Click ****Copy URL.****
3. Click ****Done.****
   ****![Confirmation screen for published Mobile Terms of Service with URL link and Copy URL button.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/30080344893339)****
4. Paste the URL wherever you want to link to your mobile terms of service.

Customers who click this link will see plain-text mobile TOS, similar to the one shown below. To preview this page, select the 3 dots then click ****View webpage****.

![Example of a mobile terms of service hosted by Klaviyo.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716055505947)

You can update this text at any time. Simply navigate to the **Mobile Terms of Service** section, make your edits, and then click ****Publish****.

[Skip to the example of a mobile TOS](#h_01GJDDBBZDETEA7W96YB3XS66E).

### Add to an existing TOS

This is for anyone who already has a TOS and simply wants to include language around a mobile or SMS program, or who wants to host their mobile TOS on their own branded page.

In this case, you will need to copy the generated text, go to and edit the page where you host your TOS, and then paste this language.

To get started:

1. Click ****Save**** after you check the acknowledgement box in step 9.
2. Click ****Save****.
3. Copy the generated updated Mobile Terms of Service text.
   ![Confirmation modal for updated Terms of Service where you can copy the updated Mobile Terms of Service to add to your site.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/30080344895387)
4. Select ****Done****.
5. Navigate to your website/ecommerce platform.
6. Find the page with your existing TOS.
7. Paste in the copied text and make any other edits you need to make.
8. Save the page.
