You must be an Owner or Admin to integrate WhatsApp and Klaviyo.

Get started with connecting your WhatsApp Business account to Klaviyo. You can automatically reach out to customers via WhatsApp at key points in their customer journey.

![](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/zflrjg8atj/swatch)

## Before you begin

Before starting, please make sure you:

- Prepare your company information, including:
  - Legal business name and address.
  - Legal business website. The URL must include your business domain.
  - Desired display name in WhatsApp.
- Have your credentials to Facebook and a Meta Business account.
  - Please note that Meta may take up to several hours to verify new accounts.
- Consider the phone number to use for WhatsApp (e.g., main business or support phone number)
  - This cannot be your SMS sending number.

## Find WhatsApp in Klaviyo

1. Click your account name in the lower left corner.
2. Select ****Settings > WhatsApp****.
3. Click ****Connect to WhatsApp**** to get started.
4. Review the permissions.
   ![Permissions to connect WhatsApp to Klaviyo](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40648480055323)
5. When ready, click ****Get started****.

## Log in to your Facebook account

To use or connect WhatsApp, you must have a Facebook account.

****Don’t already have a Facebook account? Set one up.****

If you do not have a Facebook account, click ****Create an account**** and follow the steps from the embedded sign-up to create an account. For more details, check out this [Facebook setup article](https://www.facebook.com/help/188157731232424).

Once you set up your Facebook account, you must wait 1 hour before continuing.

1. Enter your Facebook username or email as well as your password.
2. Choose to ****Continue as [Your Facebook account]****.

- If the name of the account isn’t correct, click ****Cancel**** and try again.

If you have trouble logging in to your account, see [Facebook’s login and password assistance guides](https://www.facebook.com/help/292105707596942/).

The next steps require that you have both a:

- Meta Business account
- WhatsApp Business profile

  - Click ****Create a business account**** and enter the following information:
    - Company name.
    - Professional email address (used to verify the account).
    - Business website or profile page
    - Country
    - Address (optional)

## Choose a phone number for WhatsApp

1. Enter the phone number for your WhatsApp account by either:

   - ****Using your business or support phone number****
   - You can use an existing number, such as your main company or support phone number, for WhatsApp. Alternatively, you can set up a Google Voice number for WhatsApp.
     - You cannot use your SMS phone number from Klaviyo.
   - ****Getting a free number from Meta****
   - You can request a free phone number from Meta. To get approved, Meta must review your business and display name. This may take up to 5 business days.
   - ****Migrating a number already on WhatsApp****
   - If you have a phone number registered with WhatsApp Messenger or WhatsApp Business App, you can [migrate this phone number](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/get-started/migrate-existing-whatsapp-number-to-a-business-account/).
   - ****Migrating a number from another Solution Partner****
   - You can [migrate your business phone numbers from another Solution Partner](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/40116637850651) by migrating it from an existing WhatsApp Business Account to the one you plan to use with Klaviyo. When you do this, you retain your display names, quality ratings, template messaging limits, Official Business Account statuses, and approved templates.![image.png](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40650023720219)
2. Pick how you want to verify this number (either text message or phone call).

   - If using a Google Voice number, choose to verify by a phone call.
3. Click ****Next****.
4. Enter the 6-digit code to verify your phone number.

Newly created WhatsApp Business accounts must be verified within 30 days. If you created a new WhatsApp business account, see instructions for verifying your WhatsApp account.
