## You will learn

Learn about the actions available for your campaigns before, during, and after sending. Note that, depending on the status of your campaign, some options may not be available. For example, a sent campaign cannot be edited. However, you can edit a draft campaign, or a scheduled campaign that has been paused.

## Delete a campaign

Campaigns in draft mode (i.e., not yet scheduled or sent) can be deleted. Scheduled, sending, and sent campaigns cannot be deleted, but you can [archive them](#h_01G1KPF8SFKZ5WCYJTDQERY27W).

Once deleted, a campaign cannot be recovered.

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Click the 3-dot menu to the right of the campaign you would like to delete.
3. Click ****Delete****.
   ![Option to delete a campaign](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34640475377435)
4. Click ****Delete campaign**** in the modal to confirm and delete.

## Edit campaign content

You can edit any campaign that hasn’t yet been sent (including campaigns that are scheduled to send at a future time, as long as you pause them first). Messages that have already been sent cannot be edited.

### Edit a draft or paused campaign

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Click on the draft campaign you would like to edit, or select the 3 dots on the right-hand side and click ****Edit campaign****.
3. Update as desired.

### Edit a scheduled campaign

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Click the 3 dots to the far right of the campaign you'd like to edit.
3. Click ****Pause**** to pause the campaign.
   ![Pause a campaign](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723518982043)
4. Update as desired.

Learn more about [pausing campaigns](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411545074203).

### Edit a partially sent campaign

If you notice an error in a campaign that is mid-send, you can cancel the campaign to make adjustments.

Note that the messages that have already been sent cannot be edited. Canceling a campaign mid-send and resending it will only correct the mistake for recipients who were queued for sending, but had not yet received it.

There are three main steps to editing a campaign mid-send:

1. [Cancel the campaign.](#h_01G1KPHSASTAWAABRAVSKQ0997)
2. [Create a segment](#h_01G1KPJ4BE4VB0504XDDYF5HZX) of the recipients who already received the campaign.
3. [Edit and resend the campaign](#h_01G1KPJDG7DS9PYS2S5HA6B545) to those who have not yet received it, excluding the segment of initial recipients.

#### Cancel the campaign

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Click the three dots icon to the far right of the campaign you'd like to cancel.
3. Click ****Cancel****.

![Cancel a campaign](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723518986267)

#### Identify those who have already received the campaign

1. Navigate to the ****Lists & Segments**** tab.
2. Click ****Create List/Segment****.
3. Click ****Segment****.
4. Create a segment with the following definition: ****What someone has done > Received Email > at least once over all time > where campaign name equals \_\_\_****.
   ![A segment definition to locate people who have already received a message](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723507345947)
5. Click ****Create Segment****.

Make sure to select the name of your campaign from the dropdown menu that appears.

#### Edit and resend the campaign

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Open the three dots icon for the canceled campaign.
3. Click ****Clone > Clone to this account****.
4. Apply your edits to the cloned version of the campaign.
5. Select the segment you created in the last step in the **Don’t send to** field.
   ![On the campaigns page, the segment from the section above is excluded](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723507347867)
6. Send the campaign.

## Reschedule a campaign

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Click the name of the campaign you’d like to reschedule.
3. On the following page, click ****Reschedule****.
   ![Reschedule a campaign](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723507362843)
4. Select a new send time.
5. Click ****Schedule**** to apply the changes.

## Rename a campaign

You cannot rename a campaign that has already been sent.

To change the name of a campaign:

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Click the additional options menu (3 dots) next to the campaign you'd like to rename.
3. Click ****Edit details****.
4. On the following screen, edit the name in the **Campaign name** field.
5. Click ****Save****.

## View a campaign

Once a campaign has been sent, you cannot edit any part of the campaign or campaign settings, but you can view the content.

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Find the campaign you would like to view and click the three dots icon to the right of the campaign name.
3. Click ****View Campaign****.

![View a campaign](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723507326619)

## Archive a campaign

You can archive any campaign that has been sent or canceled. Draft campaigns can be [deleted](#h_01G1KPD88DV12H2CGNT7YRZP91), but not archived. Archived campaigns won't show in the campaigns list view unless you filter for them.

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Find the campaign you would like to archive and click the three dots icon to the right of the campaign name.
3. Click ****Move to archive****.

![Move a campaign to the archive](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723507330587)

To view your archived campaigns, click the ****Archived**** filter from the filter bar above the campaigns list view.

## Unarchive a campaign

You can filter to see all of your archived campaigns within Campaigns tab. To view and unarchive an archived campaign:

1. Click the ****Archived**** filter. Navigate to the ****Options**** dropdown menu in the upper right-hand corner of your screen.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34640459734555)
2. Click the three dots icon to the right of the campaign name and select ****Unarchive**** to unarchive your campaign.

## View summary details for a scheduled campaign

To review the details of a scheduled campaign (including the send time, message content, send lists, and more):

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Click the name of any scheduled campaign.
3. On the following page, review the message’s details.

If needed, you can pause the campaign to make edits.

## Copy a campaign ID

To copy a campaign's ID:

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. From the list or calendar view, click the additional options button next to a campaign.
3. Click ****Copy campaign ID****.
