## You will learn

Learn about your options when viewing, filtering, and sorting your campaigns. Klaviyo offers several display options for your campaigns, making it easy to understand your sending cadence and find campaigns that meet certain criteria.

## View your campaigns

There are 2 ways to view your campaigns which can be toggled in the campaigns tab:

- List view
- Calendar view

Use either view to see your past and upcoming campaigns, along with summary details like the campaign’s open and click rate. Open rate is not tracked in Klaviyo for SMS, so you will always see a dash in place of a percentage for that channel. For push notifications, you’ll see the open rate, but there will be a dash for the click rate.

## List view

1. Navigate to your ****Campaigns**** tab using the navigation sidebar.
   ![The Campaigns tab in Klaviyo](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717992433563)
2. View your campaigns and the summary details, or hover over a campaign’s name for a more detailed view.
   ![Campaigns dashboard with filters and email campaign list showing draft status, dates, and other information.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/32567747595675)

   To change which columns are visible in the list view:
3. Click the column filter icon on the far left of the filter bar.
4. Toggle the visibility of each column. All columns will be on by default.

![Column customization panel with toggles for Last updated, Message type, Status, Open rate, Click rate, and Conversion metric](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471940297755)

## Calendar view

1. Navigate to your ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. Click the calendar icon in the top right.
   ![Toggle switch interface showing the calendar view option selected](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471940298267)
3. Click any campaign name to view a summary of the campaign’s details, including open and click rates if available.

![Calendar view of campaigns for December 2024 showing example SMS campaigns scheduled on various dates](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471980908187)

## Filter your campaigns

There are several options for filtering campaigns:

- Search bar
- Date range
- Audience
- Channels
- Status
- Tags
- A/B test
- Archive
- Conversion metric

![Campaign filter bar with date range, audience, channels, status, tags, A/B test, archived, and placed order options](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471980908571)

### Search bar

Use the search bar in the top left-hand side of your screen to search for a specific campaign name in list view. Search is not available in the calendar view.

For instance, if you are looking for a campaign called “Summer Launch 2025,” you can type in any part of this title to find the campaign(s) with that word in the name.

![Search campaigns input field](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471940299931)

### Date range

Select a date range to filter for campaigns that were sent within a specific time period. Scheduled and draft campaigns are always displayed, regardless of date.

Choose between one of the following:

- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 90 days
- Year-to-date
- Last year
- All time
- Custom range

  To define a custom range, do either of the following:
- Type a start date and end date in the field below the custom range option.
- Click a start date and end date in the calendar on the right.

![Date range selector showing preset options like Last 7 days, Last 30 days, and others with December 2024 calendar](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471940300827)

### Audience

Use the list filter to view only campaigns sent to a certain list or segment. If you choose multiple lists or segments, you’ll see any campaign sent to one or more of the selected lists/segments.

![Audience filter dropdown showing example list options in an account](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471980910107)

### Channels

There are 3 types of campaigns available in Klaviyo:

- Email
- SMS
- Push
  ![Channels filter dropdown with Email, SMS, and Push notification options](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471940303643)

  Email is enabled for your account by default, but SMS and push must be activated in order to appear as selectable options for campaigns.

  Learn [How to set up SMS in Klaviyo](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404274419355).

  For push, there are 3 options:
- [Android](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/14750928993307)
- [iOS](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023213971)
- [ReactNative](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/22344173696539)

### Status

Filter your campaigns based on their sending status:

- Sent
- Scheduled
- Draft
- Cancelled

![Campaign list filtered to show sent email campaigns with green sent status indicators](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471980910875)

Easily see the campaigns that subscribers have already received so you can review results, or filter for draft campaigns so you know what work still needs to be completed.

### Tags

Choose from custom tags you can create to organize your campaigns. [Learn about tags in Klaviyo](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040327431).

![Tags filter dropdown showing an account's tagging options: important, promotion, simple, test](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471980912283)

### A/B test

View only campaigns that have an A/B test set up.

![A/B test filtered campaigns showing email campaigns with A/B designation and draft status](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471940305563)

### Archive

View campaigns that have been archived.

![Campaign list with placed order filter showing metrics like open rate (100.00%) and click rate (0.00%)](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471980912539)

### Conversion metric

Change the conversion metric displayed in the last column of the list view. Available metrics depend on your installed integrations.

If you change the conversion metric, it will change for all users of your account.

![Placed Order filter options including Checkout Started, Fulfilled Order, Ordered Product, and Active on Site](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/37471940306203)
