Learn about the questions feature included in Klaviyo Reviews, which allows your team to answer and display user-submitted product questions.

## About questions

Collect and review user-submitted product questions and enable your team to display answers on the relevant product page. When you add a reviews widget to your product pages, a **Questions** tab is included by default.

![Reviews questions tab](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717418203675)

Site visitors can click the ****Ask a question**** button to submit a question and provide their name and email as contact information.

## How to view questions

To see the questions that have been submitted:

1. Select ****Reviews**** in the Klaviyo sidebar.
2. Click ****Q&A****.
   ![The questions tab](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717418207259)
3. Note any unanswered questions, which are marked as **Pending**. Questions your team has already reviewed will either be marked as **Answered** or **Rejected**.

![An example review quesiton](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717390949659)

## How to answer questions

To answer customer questions, navigate to ****Reviews > Q&A****, then click ****Answer**** next to any unanswered questions. Note that answering a question will notify the person who submitted a question via the email address they provided.

If you’d like to share your answer directly with the person who asked it, click the **i** icon in the question card. From this page, you can locate their email address and contact them directly.

![The more info page for a question](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717418205595)

If a question is inappropriate or you don’t want to display it on your site, click ****Reject****. Rejected questions are stored in the **Q&A** tab and are not visible to site visitors.

## Get notified about new questions

Set up email notifications whenever someone asks a question about one of your products.

Enabling notifications for new questions means you’ll also be notified whenever someone submits a review. It is not possible to subscribe exclusively to product question notifications.

1. Navigate to the ****Reviews**** tab in Klaviyo.
2. Click ****Reviews settings****.
3. Select ****Publishing rules and notifications****.
4. Under **Notifications**, check the box next to **Pending activity (reviews and Q&As)**.
5. Choose a frequency (immediately, daily, or weekly) and delivery timing.
   ![reviews notifications settings](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717418211739)
6. Choose one or more notification recipients from your list of account users. Notifications will be sent to them when someone submits a review or product question.

   Notifications can only be sent to account users. If you’d like to send notifications to someone you don’t see in the dropdown, [invite them to join your Klaviyo account](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053547071).
7. Optionally, check the box next to **Weekly stats summary** to receive a weekly report on your product reviews. This can be sent to a different user (or users) than those who are selected for review notifications.
8. Click ****Save changes****.
