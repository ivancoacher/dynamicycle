Learn how to display a customer review on a Klaviyo sign-up form to build trust with your site visitors and encourage more submissions.

You can choose between adding a store review or specific product review to showcase on your sign-up form and customize the styling to match your brand.

## Before you begin

If this is your first time creating a sign-up form, learn the basics of [getting started with sign-up forms](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026474752).

If you haven’t yet, make sure to set up [Klaviyo Reviews](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/15937542819355).

## Add the review block to your form

1. From Klaviyo's main navigation, select ****Sign-up forms****.
2. Click ****Create form****. From here you can either:

   - Customize a pre-built template from the form library
   - Click ****Create blank form**** in the top right corner to start from scratch.
3. In the form editor, click ****Add blocks**** from the **Overview** sidebar.
4. Select the ****Review quote**** block and drag the block onto your form.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/36635960432155)
5. Click ****Add review****.
6. Select either ****Store reviews**** or ****Product reviews****.
7. Browse the list of available reviews and click ****Select**** next to the one you want to display.

![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/36635960444187)

You can show multiple reviews by adding more review blocks to your form. However, we recommend displaying no more than 2–3 reviews to keep your form clean and focused.

## Review filter options

When choosing a review to display, there are several options for filtering through reviews, including:

- ********Search********
  Search for reviews containing a specific word or phrase.
- ****Rating****
  Filter by number of stars.
- ********Verified********
  Show only verified customers.
- ****Exclude incentivized****
  Omit reviews given in exchange for incentives.
- ****Time period****
  Limit display to reviews submitted within a specified timeframe.
- ********Length********
  Limit display to reviews with a specified length.

![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/36635960446363)

## Edit review block appearance

After adding a review block and selecting a review quote to display, you can toggle the following:

- ********Rating********
  Whether or not to show the star rating.
- ****Reviewer name****
  Whether or not to show the reviewer’s first name and first letter of their last name, e.g. James S.
- ****Verified badge****
  Whether or not to show the verified checkmark next to the reviewer’s name if it is a verified review.
  ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/36635973728539)

  You can also adjust the styling for the following sections to make sure the font and colors align with your brand:
- Rating
- Quote text
- Reviewer name text
- Verified badge style
- Block padding
