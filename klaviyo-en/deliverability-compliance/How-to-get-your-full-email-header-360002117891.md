## You will learn

Learn what an email header is and how to get it depending on your inbox provider.

Our Compliance Team sometimes requests that you provide us an email header so we can better troubleshoot and investigate specific issues.

## What is an email header?

An email header contains a lot of information about an email and the network path it has taken. This type of information helps our Compliance Team narrow down issues. All emails have an email header and all email clients pull certain information from the header and display it in a user-friendly way. The type of information you can find includes the from address, from/reply-to email address, subject line, recipient, and more.

## Gmail.com (works for G-Suite/Google apps)

First, open the email you would like to get the header for and click the down arrow in the upper right corner of the message, next to the reply button.

Select the option ****Show original.****

![Email in Gmail inbox with the drop-down menu open from the down arrow in the upper right corner of the message](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335231259)

A new tab will open displaying the original message. Copy and paste the message or download the original and send it to us directly.

![Original message tab in Gmail displaying info about the message.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335229467)

## AOL

Open the email you would like to get the header for and select ****More > View Message Source.****

![View message source option for email in in AOL inbox](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713329538587)

A new window will open displaying the raw header. Copy and paste this and send it to us directly.

![Window displaying the raw header for email in AOL inbox](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335210907)

## Outlook / Hotmail / Outlook 365 (Web-Email)

Open the email you would like to get the header for and click the options dropdown to the right of the from name/email address.

Select ****View message source.****

![View message source option for emails in Outlook and Hotmail inboxes ](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335207067)

The full email header will display in a modal. Copy and paste this and send it to us directly.

![Message source window for emails in Outlook and Hotmail inboxes](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713329530779)

## Yahoo

Open the email you would like to get the header for and select the option ****More > View Raw Message****.

![View raw message option for email in Yahoo inbox](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335201819)

A new tab will open with the raw header. Copy/paste this and send it to us directly.

![Window with raw header for email in Yahoo inbox](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335193115)

## Apple Mail (Mac OS)

Select the email you would like to get the header for and click ****View**** from the top menu bar.

Select ****Message > Raw Source.****

![Raw source option for email in Apple Mail inbox](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335233691)

A new window will open with the raw header. Copy and paste this and send it to us directly.

![Window with raw header for email in Apple Mail inbox](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28713335188763)
