## You will learn

Learn how to change your account name (also called your organization name) in Klaviyo. Knowing how to do this is helpful if your company is rebranding, there’s a mistake in the current name, or you simply want to edit it in some way.

## Before you begin

Please note the following:

- You must be an Owner or Admin to edit the account’s name.
- If you use a branded sender ID to send SMS, you may also want to change that ID.
  - In Australia, you will also need to [register the new branded sender ID](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/14953787622427).

## Change account name in Klaviyo

1. Click the current account name in the bottom left corner.
2. Select ****Settings****.
   ![Account dropdown in bottom left when Settings is highighted](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723545985179)
3. Under **Account**, choose ****Organization****.
   ![Organization tab under Account Settings](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723545991835)
4. Review and, if needed, edit the **Default sender name**.
5. Under **Company/organization name**, input your desired account name. Here, we changed “Bridge Designs” to “Designs by Bridge” and also updated the sender name.
   ![Organization tab, showing where to change the company name, sender name, and more](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723545990043)
6. Click ****Save****.
7. Refresh the page or click into a new tab to see your new account name.
   ![Account dropdown showing the new organization name](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723524059931)
