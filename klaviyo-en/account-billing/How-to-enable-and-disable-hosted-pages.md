## You will learn

Learn how to turn on hosted pages in your Klaviyo account. You can use hosted pages to display custom-coded consent pages to your subscribers, rather than using [Klaviyo's default consent pages](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005251848).

This article explains how to turn on the hosted pages setting in your account.

- If you're instead looking for more information on what hosted pages do, see [understanding custom hosted pages](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360057676272).
- For guidance on creating custom hosted pages once you've enabled them, see [How to build custom hosted pages.](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005077067)

## Turn on hosted pages

Hosted pages are available for paid accounts who have passed account verification. If you do not fit this criteria, you will not see the ability to enable this setting.

Before you can replace one of your account's default consent pages, you must enable hosted pages. To enable hosted pages:

1. Click your organization name in the bottom left corner.
2. Navigate to ****Settings > Other > Consent pages****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34267032329499)
3. Select ****Enable Custom Pages**** to turn this feature on and add the Hosted Pages tab to your account.

Next, head to [how to custom code consent pages](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005077067#h_01J5970D8JJCQ078TWYT1ADHV9) to begin creating your custom page.

## Disable hosted pages

If you want to turn off or remove a hosted page, do not delete the page HTML. Instead, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to ****Settings > Other****.
2. In the ****Consent pages**** tab, find the **Subscribe page**section.
3. Open the dropdown next to the ****Edit page**** button.
4. Select ****Remove Hosted Page****.
   ![Edit page dropdown highlighting the option to remove a hosted page](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28705637334171)
