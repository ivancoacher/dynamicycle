## Contact support

If you are on a free or paid plan, log into your Klaviyo account and use our support page at [klaviyo.com/support](https://www.klaviyo.com/support).

If you are locked out of your account or have an invoice inquiry, please submit a ticket though our [request form](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/requests/new).

## Support for free vs. paid plans

We offer email and live chat support to customers on all paid plans.

Customers on free plans have access to a 60-day trial of email support, along with a variety of other resources including the [Help Center](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us), [Academy](https://academy.klaviyo.com/), [Klaviyo Community](https://community.klaviyo.com/), and interactive [live trainings](https://www.klaviyo.com/events/live-training).

## How to contact support in-app

1. When you're logged in to your account, select ****Support**** in the top right of the app or navigate to [klaviyo.com/support](https://www.klaviyo.com/support).
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39792783258651)
2. Review the common questions, or select ****Email support****or ****Chat support****to contact us directly.
   - For email support: fill out and submit the form.
   - For chat support: start by asking your question to Kira, Klaviyo’s Virtual Product Expert.
     - Enter your question into the associated chatbox.
     - Kira will direct you to any applicable resources or solutions.
     - If Kira is unable to answer your question and you are on a paid plan, you will be added to our live chat queue, where you will be able to speak with a member of our support team.

If you aren't able to access email or chat support within the **Need further assistance?** section of the widget, you can access them by clicking the ****Support**** link to [klaviyo.com/support](https://www.klaviyo.com/support).

## Unable to access chat?

Some ad-blocking software or browser add-ons may prevent the chat option from appearing in your account dropdown menu.

If you're on a paid plan and you're experiencing difficulties accessing chat, try disabling any ad blockers or browser add-ons.

If the problem persists, reach out to our success team by clicking ****Support > Email support****.

## Unable to log into your Klaviyo account?

If you have been locked out or are unable to log into your Klaviyo account, please submit a ticket through our [account access request form](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/requests/new). From here, a member of our support team will assist in retrieving your access.
