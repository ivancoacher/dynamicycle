## You will learn

Learn about the built-in integrations offered by Klaviyo that will allow you to sync data with your new Klaviyo account and use this information for precision targeted marketing.

## Ecommerce integrations

You should integrate with your ecommerce platform to pull in data from your store, like **Placed Order, Checkout Started**, and other events. Which events you're able to sync with Klaviyo will depend on your particular platform:

- [Shopify](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005080407?flash_digest=80fadbe9b222aac3d6ce509489218162f4195fc5)
- [Magento 1.x](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005082187-Integrate-with-Magento-1-x)
- [Magento 2.x](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005254348-Integrate-with-Magento-2-x)
- [BigCommerce](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005082547-Integrate-with-BigCommerce)
- [WooCommerce](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005255808-Integrate-with-WooCommerce)
- [Wix](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/6202669053723)
- [OpenCart](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005255408-Integrate-with-OpenCart)
- [PrestaShop](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360054551492)
- [Spree](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005255448-Integrate-with-Spree)
- [Shift4Shop (formerly 3dcart)](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005083107-Integrate-with-3dcart)
- [Volusion](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005083427-Integrate-with-Volusion)

If you don't see your ecommerce platform listed, check out our [guide to integrating a platform without a pre-built Klaviyo integration](https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/guide_to_integrating_a_platform_without_a_pre_built_klaviyo_integration).
