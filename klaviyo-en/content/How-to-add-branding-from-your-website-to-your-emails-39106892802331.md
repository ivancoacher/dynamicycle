Learn how to use the brand library to set up branding for your emails in Klaviyo. The brand library can detect your branding directly from your website, or you can upload content manually.

Detecting branding from your website is available for new accounts, if you’ve already updated your branding previously and would like to learn more, learn [how to update your brand styles in Klaviyo](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403537778331).

The brand library can automatically detect and collect the following elements from your website:

- Logos
- Colors
- Buttons
- Fonts

## Add branding from your website

When you’re setting up branding for the first time, you’ll see the option to automatically detect branding from your website and upload it to Klaviyo.

1. Navigate to ****Content > Images & brand****.
2. Click ****Guide me****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39106892778779)
3. Select **Detect from my website** and enter your website’s URL.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39106898203675)
4. Click ****Next****.
5. Review the brand elements that were detected. If there are any you’d like to exclude, click them to deselect. Otherwise, click ****Next****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39106892788123)
6. Set up branding for your email templates. The header, footer, and body will be automatically populated with elements from the previous step, but you can manually change them as desired.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39106898209179)
7. Click ****Next****.
8. If you’re satisfied with your branding, click ****Exit****. Otherwise, click ****Back**** to return to previous steps.

## Add branding manually

If you don’t have a website set up or you’d like to upload everything yourself:

1. Navigate to ****Content > Images & brand****.
2. Click ****Guide me > Add branding manually****.
3. In the editor, add elements to the header, body, and footer to the branding of your email templates.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/39106898212891)
4. Click ****Next****.
5. If you’re satisfied with your branding, click ****Exit****.

## Update your brand style settings

Once you’ve set up your branding in the brand library, you can update your branding at any time.

1. Navigate to ****Content > Images & brand****.
2. Select the ****Brand**** tab to add elements to your brand such as logos, colors, and fonts.
3. Select ****Defaults**** to edit the header, body, and footer of your default email templates.

Learn more about [how to update your brand styles in Klaviyo](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403537778331).
