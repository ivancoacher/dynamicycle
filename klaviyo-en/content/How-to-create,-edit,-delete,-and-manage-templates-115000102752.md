## You will learn

Learn how to create, edit, delete, and manage your email templates in the ****Email Templates**** tab.

## Create a template

1. Navigate to ****Content > Templates****.
2. Browse the ****Email: saved**** tab and select a base template to use, or click ****Create**** to start from scratch. Alternatively, [import a custom HTML template](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005254068).
3. Review the template in the modal that appears; then, click ****Create Template****.

This takes you to the template editor where you can create and save your template.

## Edit a template

1. Navigate to ****Content > Templates****.
2. Click ****Email: saved**** to view your existing templates.
3. Click the icon of the template you’d like to edit.
4. Click ****Use template**** in the modal that appears.

This takes you to the template editor where you can edit your content and save your template.

This will not update templates that are in use in your flows or unsent campaigns.

## Change the name of a template

1. Navigate to ****Content >**** ****Templates****.
2. Locate the template you want to update.
3. Select the three dots on the far right-hand side.
4. In the dropdown, select ****Edit Name**** and rename the template. ![The Edit Name option for a template](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723505406235)

## Clone or delete a template

1. Select ****Content > Templates > Email: saved**** to locate the template you want to clone or delete.
2. Click the dropdown and select the option you want to take (i.e., ****Clone**** or ****Delete****).
   ![The Clone option for a template](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723505408923)

Deleting a template will not impact any flows or campaigns that use the template. When you use a template email in a flow or a campaign, Klaviyo makes a copy of the template, and it's no longer connected to the template found in your**Templates** tab.

## Save an email as a template

If you're working with either a draft campaign email or a flow email, you can save your email as a template. To save a campaign email as a template:

1. Click the ****Content**** step from within an existing unsent campaign.
2. Click the ****Edit Email**** dropdown.
3. Select ****Save as Template****. ****![The Save as template option](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28723505411739)****
4. Enter a name for your template and click ****Save as Template****.

Your email template is now available in the **Templates** tab.

To save a flow email as a template:

1. Click the ****Message Content**** tab.
2. Click the ****Edit Content**** dropdown.
3. Select ****Save as Template****.
4. Enter a name for your template and click ****Save as Template****.

Your email template is now available in the **Templates** tab.

## Sending templates

In Klaviyo, there are 2 main ways to send your email templates to your subscribers:

- [Use a campaign](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005054847-Guide-to-Creating-and-Sending-an-Email-Campaign) to send an email template to a group of subscribers at the same time.
- [Use a flow](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002774932-Getting-Started-with-Flows) to send an email template based on when a subscriber does something.

## Switching templates

If you're working with either a draft campaign email or any flow email, you can change the template you're working with.

1. Click the ****Content**** tab within the message (i.e., flow email or unsent campaign).
2. Click the ****Edit Email**** dropdown.
3. Select ****Change Template****.
4. Select an option: ****Drag and Drop****, ****Text Only****, or ****HTML****, then select and edit your template.
