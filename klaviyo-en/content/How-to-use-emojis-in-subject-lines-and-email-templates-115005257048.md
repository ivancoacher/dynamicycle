## You will learn

Learn about the important considerations to make when using emojis in your subject lines and email content, particularly how differently emojis display across various clients.

If a character you use isn't supported in the email client of your recipient, the recipient will see a ☐ character instead. Even if an email client is able to render the small image (i.e. the emoji) you've added to your email, the image may not render as desired. For this reason, Klaviyo recommends testing out any new emojis you'd like to use [by sending test campaigns to a preview list](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005081907) or using a tool like Litmus.

## How to use emojis in your emails

Klaviyo supports all emojis! In Klaviyo's drag-and-drop template editor and in the subject line for all emails, click the emoji icon, then choose an emoji to add it.

![The emoji icon to add emoji to a text field](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28717380321819)

You can copy and paste emojis directly into your email. A great resource for copy and pasting emojis into your templates is [Copy and Paste Emoji](http://www.copyandpasteemoji.com/).
