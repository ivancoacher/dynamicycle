## You will learn

Learn how to create a link in a Klaviyo email that allows recipients to create a draft email, open a draft text, or initiate a phone call to you with a single click.

## Link to an email address, SMS, or phone number

To create a link to contact you in a text block:

1. Highlight the text you’d like to add a link to.
2. In the **Link** field, add the a link to your contact information using one of the [formats in the section below](#h_01HQXTP7JJV2Y7Q475JVK1ECBN).
3. Replace the contact information placeholder (e.g., youremail@example.com) with the email address or phone number you'd like your recipients to contact.
   ![A link to an email address](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720770479259)

To create a link to contact you in a button or image block:

1. Click into the block you’d like to add a link to.
2. In the **URL** or **Link address** field, add the a link to your contact information using one of the [formats in the section below](#h_01HQXTP7JJV2Y7Q475JVK1ECBN).
3. Replace the contact information placeholder (e.g., [youremail@example.com](mailto:youremail@example.com)) with the email address or phone number you'd like your recipients to contact.

   ![In Klaviyo's new editor, a user adds a button that links to an email address](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/wetef5ssfe/swatch)

## Link formats for email, SMS, and phone numbers

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ****Link type**** | ****Format**** | ****Action**** |
| Email | mailto:youremail@example.com | Clicking a link with this address opens the recipient's preferred email app and creates a draft email with your address in the **To** field. |
| SMS | sms:+19999999999 | Clicking a link with this address opens the recipient's text messaging app with a draft text addressed to your phone number. |
| Phone number | tel:123-456-7890 | Clicking a link with this address opens the recipient's default phone call app with your phone number ready to dial. |

## Limitations

These links are only supported for certain ISPs and devices. For example, SMS links (i.e., sms:+19999999999) are supported in Apple Mail but are not fully supported in all versions of Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook. Additionally, recipients who open an SMS or phone number link on a desktop, rather than a mobile device, may not have a messaging or calling app installed. For this reason, it's important to make sure your phone number or email address is visible in the message you send, so recipients can manually type it in if necessary.
