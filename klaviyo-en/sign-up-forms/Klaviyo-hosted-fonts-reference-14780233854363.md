## Import a Klaviyo-hosted font

Import a Klaviyo-hosted custom font to use in your sign-up forms, consent pages, and email templates:

1. Navigate to ****Content > Images & brand > Fonts.****
2. Select ****Import Font****.
3. Choose an alternative font to import from the list of Klaviyo-hosted fonts above.
4. Fill in the font variant’s name, weight, and style (i.e., Abril-Fatface-Klaviyo-Hosted, 400, Normal).
5. Copy your font’s URL from the list and paste it in the **Source Address** field.
6. If the font you've chosen offers multiple variants, click ****Add font variant**** for however many variants you want to add to your account, then fill in the details and corresponding URL for each one.
   ![Importing a font has multiple variants prompts you to fill in the details and URL for each one.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720761523483)
7. Select a fallback font.
8. Click ****Add Font****.

Following this process will add the font to **Your Fonts** section, as well as to the fonts dropdown list in the Klaviyo editors. Repeat this import process for any other Klaviyo-hosted fonts that you want added to your account.

If you wish to replace the font in any of your existing forms or email templates with your Klaviyo-hosted font, you will need to [change it manually in the editor](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047955672#h_01J5BQR0V8FG2ZQSYA7NCRCSVT).
