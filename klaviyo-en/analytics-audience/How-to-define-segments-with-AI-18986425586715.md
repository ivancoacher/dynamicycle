Learn how to build segments with AI built directly into Klaviyo’s segment builder. This tool accepts natural language inputs (i.e., normal phrases and sentences) and converts them into a Klaviyo segment.

## How to define segments with AI

This assistant creates segments using generative AI technology, however you are still responsible for the final segment definition.

1. Navigate to ****Audiences**** > ****Lists & segments****.
2. Click ****Create New > Create segment****.
3. Add a name for your segment.
4. Type out a sentence or phrase describing your desired segment directly in the condition selector. Not sure what to try? Start with the [examples below](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1T_9S0j0Ljqe5SFDiSx_F8eoybtCWrlvIgKb4sBK43EI/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.jq1bykobo0nv).
   ![Segments AI.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31964144945435)
5. After entering your desired prompt, either:

   - Click the text shown under **Define with AI**.
     or
   - Hit **Enter** on your keyboard.
6. You can edit individual conditions manually, or by selecting the **Define with AI** option when expanding a condition.
7. To start again with a new prompt, delete the generated conditions.

![Segments AI 2.jpg](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31964136561307)

## Example prompts

Not sure where to start? Try these sample inputs to see how the tool works:

- Engaged profiles last 30 days
- Unengaged profiles last 365 days
- Someone who has viewed a product and not made a purchase in the last 60 days
- Someone who has started checkout or viewed a product but never placed an order
- Everyone who lives within 100 miles of Boston, MA
