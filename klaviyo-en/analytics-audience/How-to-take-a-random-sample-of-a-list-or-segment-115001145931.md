## You will learn

Learn how to take a random sample from a list or segment in Klaviyo. This can be useful when running a contest or giveaway, or when you'd like to send a message to a random subset of an audience.

## Take a random sample of a list or segment

1. Navigate to ****Audience > Lists & segments**** tab of your account.
2. Click a list or segment.
3. In the upper right-hand corner of your screen, click either:
   - ****Manage Segment > Sample Segment Members**** (for a segment).
     or
   - ****Manage List > Sample list members**** (for a list).
     ![The Sample List Members option in Klaviyo](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/32431685889691)
4. Input how many people you would like to be included in this sample in the **Sample size** field.
   ![The sample creation modal for a list](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/32431685894683)
5. Click ****Create List****.

The new list created with the random sample of profiles will have [double opt-in](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005251108) enabled by default.
