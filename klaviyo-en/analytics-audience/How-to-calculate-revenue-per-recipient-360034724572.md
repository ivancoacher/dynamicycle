## You will learn

Learn how to calculate revenue per recipient (RPR) and understand how each of your marketing channels is performing. RPR helps you to determine the success of a campaign or flow and is a great way to review individual channel performance and opportunities for growth.

## Before you begin

Ensure that your Klaviyo account is integrated with an [ecommerce platform](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/categories/115000032731) and that you have sent a campaign or flow so that you have data to generate RPR. You can also use a custom placed order metric to determine revenue per recipient as long as that metric uses $value as a property.

We recommend that you have at least a month’s worth of campaigns to get an accurate baseline portrait of RPR.

## Revenue per recipient review

### Cumulative 30-day campaign RPR

The campaigns section contains a 30-day average of your RPR across all campaigns.

1. Navigate to the ****Campaigns**** tab.
2. In **Email Performance Review**, look for **Revenue Per Recipient** on the right-hand side. This RPR represents your average over the last 30 days with a comparison to similar industries and your performance (**Excellent**, **Fair**, or **Poor**).
   ![The campaign page with email performance metrics at the top of the page including average open rate, click rate, placed order rate, and revenue per recipient](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28705663543963)

### Individual campaign RPR

The overview dashboard provides a way to review an individual campaign’s RPR performance.

1. Navigate to ****Analytics > Dashboards****.
2. Either navigate into an existing dashboard or create a new one by choosing ****Create Dashboard****.
3. Look for the **Campaign Performance Detail** card. If you do not have this card on your dashboard, click ****Add Card**** to find it and add it to your view.
4. Find the campaign you wish to review RPR for from the list. Note that you may need to use the arrows at the bottom of the card to find older campaigns.
5. Once you find the campaign you want to analyze, review the **Rev/Rec** column; this is your RPR for that campaign.
   ![Campaign performance detail card with page arrows and numbers at bottom highlighted](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28705636771867)
6. Once you find the campaign you want to analyze, review the **Rev/Rec** column; this is your RPR for that particular campaign.

![Campaign performance detail card with rev/rec column on right highlighted](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28705636773787)

RPR or Rev/Rec is determined by the revenue generated from your campaign divided by the number of deliveries in that campaign. If you had campaign messages that were unable to be delivered, they are not counted in this number.

### Individual flow RPR

The overview dashboard provides a way to review an individual flow’s RPR performance.

1. 1. Navigate to ****Analytics > Dashboards****.
   2. Either navigate into an existing dashboard or create a new one by choosing ****Create Dashboard****.
   3. Look for the **Flow Performance Detail** card. If you do not have this card on your dashboard, click ****Add Card**** to find it and add it to your view.
   4. Find the flow you wish to review RPR for from the list. Note that you may need to use the arrows at the bottom of the card to find older flows.

![Flows performance detail card with page arrows and numbers at bottom highlighted](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28705663548187)

5. Once you find the flow you want to analyze, review the **Rev/Rec** column; this is your RPR for that particular campaign.
![Flows performance detail card with rev/rec column on right highlighted](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28705663549083)

RPR or Rev/Rec is determined by the revenue generated from your flows divided by the number of deliveries. If you had flow messages that weren’t delivered, they are not counted in this number.
