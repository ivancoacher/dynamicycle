## You will learn

Learn more about the Custom Reports Library and how to access it in Klaviyo. The Custom Reports Library is a repository of pre-built reports designed to answer the business questions that matter most to you. You can either browse all options or refine your search to access the report that fits your needs.

## The Custom Reports library

1. To access the Custom Reports library, head to ****Analytics > Custom Reports****.

2. If you have never built a report before, you will see a landing page with more information about our custom reports capabilities. Here, click ****Create Custom Report**** or select ****Getting Started**** to launch one of our most popular pre-built reports.

![Inside the reports library in custom reports with Create Custom Report button at top and popular business questions below](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720893834395)

You will then access the main reports page. If you are a returning user, this is also the landing page that you will access anytime you navigate to Custom Reports.

3. In the upper right-hand corner of your screen, you can either create a report from scratch or select a pre-built report. To use a pre-built report, click ****Reports Library****.

![The top area of the Analytics section showing the Reports Library button in the upper righthand size.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720893828379)

When browsing this library, you can refine your search by selecting common questions that apply to your use case.

These questions are bucketed into three categories:

- ****Message Performance****
  To hone in on how marketing messages perform in Klaviyo
- ****Channel Performance****
  To focus on how specific channels (i.e., email or SMS) perform in Klaviyo
- ****Business Performance****
  To view how specific business metrics and products perform

4. Click into any of these categories to expand a list of questions to choose from.

5. Then, click the checkbox next to any of the questions that apply to you and your use case for generating a report. This will refine your view so that only reports matching these questions will appear in your search.

![The Reports Library showing a tile for each type of custom prebuilt Klaviyp report you can choose to use for your data insights.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720848768923)

6. When you see a report that suits your needs, click into that report.

7. You can then either run that report as is or customize it to better fit your specific needs. To do so, add any relevant metrics, groupings, and filters.

![Prebuilt report modal allowing you to customize your report with further metric types, filters, and tags if you like.](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28720848770331)

8. After configuring your report, click ****Create Report****. This will process and bring you to your report below.

9. From here, you can click ****Export,**** to export and locally save your results.

Note for reports with large amounts of data, your report may take a bit longer to compile and run. Thus, you may not see the results right away after clicking ****Create Report****. Instead, you will see an empty table of results below with the initial message from us that we’re working on "Generating Your Results.” When it is complete, you will see a robust summary of your report. Your new report and its results are automatically saved so that you can return to your report via the Analytics tab at any time.

For more information on what each report type entails and how to customize them, head to [About Custom Reports in Klaviyo](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047725651).
