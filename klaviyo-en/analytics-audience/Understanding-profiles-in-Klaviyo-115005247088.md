## You will learn

Learn about profiles, including how to find them and the data available on the profile page. Profiles are used to keep track of all the information you have about your customers and prospects.

## About profiles in Klaviyo

Klaviyo maintains a complete record of contact for each person in your account. This means that we create and maintain a single profile for each and every one of your contacts. If, for example, someone is part of three different lists, they won't exist three times in your account. This person will simply have one dedicated profile where we record that they are part of these three lists.

## Access a profile in Klaviyo

1. Navigate to ****Audience**** > ****Profiles****.
   - **Profile counts:** Displays the number of total profiles and numbers of email-specific profiles (separated by active and suppressed profiles). The number listed for **Total profiles** represents the combined non-email profiles, active email profiles, and suppressed email profiles.
   - **Explore profiles**: Lists profiles and associated user data by last updated.
2. In the **Explore profiles** section, use the search bar to enter a user's name, email, or exact phone number.
3. Review the unique profile in Klaviyo to see:
   - The full timeline of what the profile has done (emails opened and clicked, website activity, support requests, purchases, and more).
   - Relevant personal information (basic contact information, referral data, etc.)

## Delete or merge profiles

Deleting a profile will remove the profile from Klaviyo completely and permanently delete all data associated with the profile. We recommend suppressing profiles rather than deleting them, as you can still access and learn from the data of suppressed profiles. Deleted profile data cannot be recovered.

Merging will result in a single profile for the contact, with one email address. The unique ID of the most recently updated profile will be maintained.

Note that deleting and merging profiles cannot be reversed.

To merge or delete a profile:

1. Navigate to an individual's profile.
2. Click the ****Profile actions**** button in the top-right corner.
3. Click either **Merge profile** or **Delete profile**. **![Profile actions availble in Klaviyo](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34265365927323)**

For more information, head to our guide on how to [delete, merge, and export a profile](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005073847).

## Review the profile page

There are 4 main tabs on a profile page in Klaviyo:

- ****Details****
  The **Details** tab provides a general overview of the profile you are looking at, including key information such as contact information and subscription status.
- ****Metrics and insights****
  The **Metrics and insights** section of a profile shows you information about a profile’s performance based on a set of key metrics you select, as well as predictive analytics data.
- ****Lists and segments**** The **Lists and segments** tab shows you all the lists and segments a profile is a member of, and allows you to add the profile to new lists.
- ****Objects****
  The **Objects** tab displays all custom objects and custom object data associated with the profile.

### Contact Information

You can see the contact information and timezone for a profile in the header of the profile page. This header with contact information is visible across all the tabs on the profile page.

![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/38085303665819)

To update someone's contact information,:

1. Click on the ****Profile actions**** button in the top-right corner of the profile page.
2. Select ****Edit contact information.****

Note that you won't be able to edit someone's location or timezone information. Learn more about [how Klaviyo populates location information and keeps it up-to-date](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005073907).

![Edit contact info modal](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716329010715)

### Activity log

Each individual profile features an activity log to capture a timeline of their interactions with your business.

The activity log appears on the right side of the page, across all tabs on the profile page.
The default view includes all events, but you can choose to view activity around a specific set of up to 5 metrics from the associated dropdown instead. To view more detailed information about each event, you can expand it using the dropdown.

![Activity log on a profile](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716329024667)

With smaller screen sizes, the activity log has its own tab on the profile page called **Activity log.**

### Channels

On the **Details** tab, you can see whether or not a customer has consented to receive marketing communication from your business, and if so, what communication channel (i.e., email, SMS marketing, SMS transactional, or mobile push notifications) that consent pertains to.

If your customer has opted in, a green checkmark will appear next to the channel they are opted in for.

![Subscribed for email status](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716328974875)

For more information, head to [Understanding consent in profiles](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037101072).

### Custom properties and source information

Custom properties in Klaviyo allow you to store information about the contacts in your account. You can see all the properties that apply to a contact on their profile page in the **Custom properties** card on the **Details** tab.

You can have a maximum of 10 properties visible at one time, at which point pagination begins.

The additional section you'll see on the **Details** tab of a profile page is **Source Information.** This section has information that is tracked when a person first comes into contact with your brand such as:

- Klaviyo-assigned **First Active** and **Last Active** dates
- **How They Found You** information that is populated by Klaviyo based on available web tracking data
- The [**Initial source**](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005075187) property

![Source information shown on profile](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716329015323)

Head to our article on [the information section of a profile](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005247028), for more information.

### Coupons

The **Coupons** section of a profile contains information about coupons and coupon codes that a customer has received. You can see this information at the bottom of the **Details** tab**.** This section won't appear if a customer has not received any coupons.

![Coupons on Details tab for profiles](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716301640731)

If you need to resend a coupon to a customer, you can use the information in this section to resend the same coupon or unique coupon code.

### Metrics

At the top of the **Metrics** **and insights** tab on a profile, you will see a card titled **Metrics**. Here you can view highlighted metrics, which can be selected using the **Edit metrics** button. The **Metrics and insights** section will show information around your highlighted metrics activity from the last 30 days, as well as all-time metrics.

![Metrics card on profile page](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716301666459)

### Predictive analytics

Please note that you will only see this section if:

- At least 500 customers have placed an order. This does not refer to active profiles, but rather the number of people who have actually made a purchase with your business. If this section is on a profile but is blank, this means Klaviyo does not have enough data on that individual to make a prediction.
- You have an ecommerce integration (e.g., Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento) or use our API to send placed orders.
- You have at least 180 days of order history and have orders within the last 90 days.
- You have at least some customers who have placed 3 or more orders.

The **Predictive Analytics** section of a profile displays the contact's customer lifetime value (CLV). You can find predictive analytics on the **Metrics and insights** tab.

Specifically, this section displays:

- Total CLV, broken into:
  - Historic CLV
  - Predicted CLV
- Expected date of next order
- Average time between orders
- Churn risk prediction
- Average order value (AOV)

![Predictive analytics shown on metrics and insights tab](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34265365936667)

Read more about [predictive analytics](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020919731) including definitions of specific fields and information on how they are calculated.

### Recently viewed products

If you have viewed product tracking installed on your website, you can see a customer’s recently viewed products on their profile page. You can see a profile’s recently viewed products at the bottom of the **Metrics and insights** tab. If a profile has not viewed any products or you do not have viewed product tracking installed, this section does not appear.

![Recently viewed products for profile on metrics and insights tab](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/28716328999963)

### Lists and segments

The **Lists and segments** tab shows all the lists and segments someone belongs to.

For each list, you can manually remove the profile by clicking ****Remove****. It is not possible to manually remove an individual from a particular segment because segments are dynamic and constrained by a set of conditions.

![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/38085351139099)

### Objects

This section and associated data only appears if:

- Your account includes custom objects. At a minimum, access to this feature requires a paid email plan or an Advanced KDP trial, which you can request through your CSM. For more on gaining access to this feature, review the [account requirements](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/35105337172123#h_01JPSTEQJQBH1MVDV2EVF5XQ9H).
- You have uploaded and mapped custom object data into Klaviyo via API. To begin working with custom objects, [create an object of your own](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/35105337172123).

The **Objects** tab displays all custom object records that pertain to a profile. You can switch between objects using the dropdown menu above the object cards.

![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/38085351141531)

To view a full object record, select the 3 dot menu and click ****View.**** A side panel will appear that contains the full object record.

## Review a profile's messages

If you click on the ****View messages**** button in the upper right-hand corner of a profile, you will be taken to their personal inbox in Klaviyo. Here, you can review all scheduled, sent, and skipped messages.

To design and send a [personal email to a profile](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005246328-Email-a-Single-Person-using-Klaviyo) select ****Send email.
![Messages tab on a profile with received messages](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34265352740379)****

There are 3 tabs within a profiles personal inbox:

- Scheduled: shows the **Scheduled At** date as well as the message's status.
- Sent: shows the status of each mesage, as well as whether an message has been opened or clicked.
- Skipped: shows all messages that were initially queued up for this person to receive, but did not send; you will see a **Processed At** date, as well as a status that reveals why the message was skipped.

Note that the skipped event is only shown on a profile's activity log if the skip was due to a send failure.

You can easily differentiate campaign and flow messages here. Campaigns are identified by the campaign name. For flows, you will see the format "Flow Name / Flow Email Name" where both are hyperlinked.
