You will learn

Learn how to view and configure the settings for a flow email which includes settings such as Smart Sending and UTM tracking.

Review a flow email’s settings

  1. Click on an email card within a flow.
  2. View the Settings section at the bottom of the righthand sidebar. You'll see the following settings:
    • Skip recently emailed profiles
      Decide whether you would like to turn Smart Sending on or off for a particular email, which skips recently emailed profiles.
    • Enable UTM Tracking 
      Decide whether you would like to turn on UTM Tracking for a particular email. 
    • Additional filters
      Add additional filters that apply exclusively to a particular email. This can be a useful tool if you would like to restrict an email to a particular group of people without creating an entirely separate path for them to go down using a conditional split.
