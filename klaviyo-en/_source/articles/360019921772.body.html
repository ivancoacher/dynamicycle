You will learn

Learn how to include a reminder of the items someone has recently viewed or added to their cart in an email. 

Before you begin

Before taking the following steps, make sure: 

  • You’ve synced your product catalog with Klaviyo. Most of Klaviyo’s ecommerce integrations (such as Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, etc.) sync your catalog automatically. 
  • You have Viewed Product tracking enabled. 

Create a personalized product feed

To create a product feed that displays someone’s recently viewed products: 

  1. From Klaviyo’s sidebar, click Content > Products.
  2. Click Manage Product Feeds.
  3. Click Create Product Feed.
  4. Name the product feed something descriptive, like RECENTLY_VIEWED_PRODUCTS. Note that spaces are not permitted in feed names.
  5. In the What products should the customers view first? dropdown, scroll down and click Products a customer has recently viewed. Alternatively, select Products a customer has added to cart for a feed of items they've recently added to their cart. 
  6. Complete the additional fields as desired.
  7. Click Save Product Feed.

Add viewed items to an email

Once you’ve created your product feed, navigate to an email template. You can add recently viewed products to any email template (including those in campaigns and flows). Once you’ve opened the editor for the template you’d like to work with: 

  1. Drag a Product block into your template.
  2. In the Content settings, make sure the feed is set to Dynamic, then choose the feed you created in the Product feed field.
  3. Modify the other options in the Styles tab to adjust how your viewed products appear, including how many products to display.
  4. When you’re finished, click Done and save your template.

Outcome

When this message is sent, recipients will see the products they viewed (or added to cart) most recently in the product block section of the email. If a recipient has a limited history of viewed products, they’ll see bestselling or most trending products, depending on the options selected in your feed settings. 

Note that Klaviyo is only able to track viewed items for cookied users.
