You will learn

Learn how to find third-party apps, which are apps built by third parties (instead of by Klaviyo) using Klaviyo's APIs. Klaviyo's APIs enable a large variety of third-parties to build apps and integrations with our platform. You can search for both Klaviyo-built integrations and select third-party apps in our app marketplace. If you are looking for an app you can't find on our marketplace, we recommend reaching out to the platform directly.

Find a third-party app

To find a third party app:

  1. In Klaviyo, select the Integrations tab.
  2. Click Explore apps.
  3. You'll be brought to our app marketplace where you can browse and search for apps.
  4. Select an app to learn more about it. 

Where to find help resources

All Klaviyo-built (or "native") integrations have help documentation on our Help Center, while third-party apps do not. You can find integration guides for Klaviyo-built integrations in the Integrations section. For third-party apps, documentation is linked on the individual app's page in our marketplace under Help resources.
