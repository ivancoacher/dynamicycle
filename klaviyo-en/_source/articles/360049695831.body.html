Klaviyo tracks when a customer opens or clicks an email using a clear pixel. By default, the pixel is located at the bottom of your emails but you can adjust its placement. If you send very large messages, then choosing to have this pixel at the top of your emails instead of the bottom can improve open tracking that is otherwise cut or clipped by some inbox providers.

For more information on why we use tracking pixels, head to Understand conversion tracking.

Edit the location of your tracking pixel

You can set the location of the tracking pixel in your emails manually.

  1. To do so, click into your account menu and select Settings.
  2. Choose Email > Tracking.
  3. Scroll down to the section called Email Tracker Position.

Click the radio button next to which option you prefer for placement, either to have the Klaviyo tracking pixel appear at the top or bottom of your emails. By default, the pixel will appear at the bottom of your emails.

By moving the tracking pixel at the bottom of your email, you could risk your email getting clipped and not tracking properly. It is advised to keep this at the top of your email if possible. In contrast, if you choose to move the pixel to the top of your message, you will see some extra spacing added that you cannot adjust. Since tracking relies on a clear pixel, this will automatically add padding to function.
