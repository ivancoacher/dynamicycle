Learn how to locate a list's subscribe link, which you can share with potential email subscribers to enable them to sign up for your email marketing. 

A subscribe page is a Klaviyo-hosted sign-up page that is automatically created for every list in your account. While you may choose to leverage popups, flyouts, or embedded forms on your website, you can also share a link to this subscribe page in emails or social media posts as an alternative way to grow your email list. 

Find the subscribe link for your list

To find the subscribe link for a list:

  1. Click the Audience dropdown in the left hand navigation.
  2. Select the Lists & Segments tab.
  3. Click into a list. Check the Type column to ensure you're selecting a list, not a segment. 

  4. Navigate to the Signup Forms tab.
  5. Scroll down to the Subscribe Page section at the bottom of the page. 
  6. If you see a list of consent pages, click Subscribe page.
    1. On the following page, in the modal to the left of the subscribe page editor, click Copy URL.
  7. If you don't see a list of consent pages, click Copy Subscribe Page URL.

After following these steps, the URL will be copied to your clipboard and you can paste it wherever you’d like.
