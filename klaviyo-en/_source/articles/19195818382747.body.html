SMS and email 

There are 3 strategies you can use with SMS and email. You can: 

  • Send an SMS first, then follow up with an email.
  • Send an email first, then follow up with an SMS. 
  • Send an email and SMS at the same time, then follow up via whichever channel the recipient engages with.

Send both SMS and email at the same time

These strategies are all good options. You can pick one and use it for all your messaging, or you can switch between them depending on the situation. 

For instance, some businesses prefer to set up their mixed SMS and email flows with email first. The reason is that flow messages (except the welcome flow) tend not to be as time sensitive as campaigns. Since you send the SMS later, this approach uses fewer credits than starting with a text message. 

Mobile push with email or SMS

In general, push should take priority over SMS or email. You want to drive people into your app and get them to engage with you again.  

It may not be a good use case for push notifications if:

  • You want the information to be referenced again. 
  • Recipients should go to your website (and not your app). 
  • You want recipients to respond and record that data in Klaviyo (review an item or answer a survey). 
  • Certain flows where a push might be seen as strange (thank you flows or canceled order).
