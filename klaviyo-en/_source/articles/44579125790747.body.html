Overview

The activity logger is a self-service audit trail that provides visibility into all actions performed within your Klaviyo account. Instead of contacting Klaviyo support to investigate account changes, you can now independently track who made a change, what that change was, and when it happened.

This transparency helps ensure account security, simplifies compliance auditing, and allows you to quickly troubleshoot configuration issues by identifying the source of specific updates.

In this article, you will learn:

  • Who can access the activity logger.
  • The benefits of using the audit trail for your business.
  • How to navigate and filter the log.

Access and permissions

To ensure account security, the activity logger is not visible to all users. It is available exclusively to:

  • Account Owners
  • Admins
  • Users with "Account Settings" permissions

Benefits of the activity logger

Using the activity logger allows you to manage your account more effectively without the need for external assistance.

  • Enhanced Visibility: Gain a clear view of user behavior across your entire organization to see "who is doing what" in real-time.
  • Streamlined Troubleshooting: Quickly resolve support or configuration issues by identifying exactly when a setting or campaign was modified.
  • Security and Compliance: Maintain a permanent record of all account edits to meet internal or external security audit requirements.
  • Self-Service Efficiency: Eliminate the need to email Klaviyo Support to verify historical account actions.

How to use the activity logger

To access the logger, navigate to Settings > Account > Activity Logger.

View edit actions

The log provides a comprehensive list of all edit actions performed in your account. Each entry includes the timestamp, the user's email address, and the category of the change.

Filter and search

Use the search and filter tools to narrow down the log by:

  • User: Track the activity of a specific team member.
  • Timeframe: Audit changes made during a specific launch or reporting period.
  • Keyword: Search for specific flow titles, segment names, or campaign IDs.

Open beta limitations

This feature is currently in open beta. While the log captures 100% of edit actions, the "Event Details" (the specific granular data about what was changed within an item) are currently limited.

We are adding these granular details incrementally as we move toward General Availability (GA).

[!TIP] Need more information? If you see an action in the logger but require more specific details that are not yet available in the beta interface, please reach out toKlaviyo Support for additional assistance.
