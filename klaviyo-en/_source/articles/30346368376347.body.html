About reviews and SEO

Search engines are built to help searchers find the content they’re looking for. To do this, search engine algorithms consider myriad factors to determine which sites are trustworthy and relevant. Collecting and featuring customer reviews on your site can help with many of these factors, including:

  • Keywords
    Reviews are keyword-rich, which helps search engines know what queries to rank your pages for.
  • Internal linking
    Highlighting reviews across your site creates a network of links among your site pages.
  • Content recency
    Because customers regularly submit new reviews, your content is continually updated.
  • Reputation
    A history of primarily positive reviews demonstrates the legitimacy of your business,

Do negative reviews hurt my SEO?

The short answer: no. All businesses will receive at least a small percentage of negative reviews. In fact, a smattering of negative reviews sprinkled among many glowing, positive reviews helps visitors feel assured that your reviews are legitimate.

Search engines won’t penalize you for a handful of poor reviews. Instead, they’ll look at the overall themes and sentiment of your reviews over time, and use that to determine your site’s trustworthiness.

The SEO widget

The SEO / All reviews widget features every single review you receive (including both product and store reviews). Search engines can crawl this page to read your most recent reviews. Create a standalone All reviews page on your site to feature this widget.

Enable rich snippets

Rich snippets allow Google to highlight product details, including a product’s star rating, directly within search results. This can make your product pages more visually appealing in search results, and gives browsers key information before they even land on your site.
