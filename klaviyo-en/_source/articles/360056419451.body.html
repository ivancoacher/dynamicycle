You will learn

Find what tax IDs Klaviyo supports as well as the specifics for your own country’s tax information. Each country has different local regulations that dictate the tax ID codes, names, and uses for tax purposes.

If you have not provided a tax ID, value added tax (VAT) or goods and services tax (GST) may be assessed depending on the local regulations of the country that your business resides in. If you provide a valid tax ID, you will not be charged VAT or GST and may be responsible for self-assessment of VAT or GST via the reverse charge mechanism. If you do not have a tax ID, the tax rate will be based primarily on the location consistent with your payment method or secondarily, your billing address.

Tax ID information by country

If your country’s tax ID is supported in Klaviyo, you can update your tax ID in the Billing Preferences page of your account. You will then be able to view this ID in all billing invoices created after you’ve added it to Klaviyo.
