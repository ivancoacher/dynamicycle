Create a WhatsApp welcome flow to automatically message new subscribers after they opt in. A welcome flow helps you make an immediate impression and guide subscribers toward their first purchase.

As a best practice, keep your WhatsApp welcome flow separate from your email welcome series.

What is a flow?

A flow is an automated series of actions or messages that send after someone performs a specific action. For a WhatsApp welcome flow, this means that after someone consents to receive WhatsApp marketing, they automatically receive messages welcoming them to your program.

Why use a WhatsApp welcome series?

WhatsApp lets you reach new subscribers in a direct and conversational way.

Because WhatsApp is an immediate channel, you can connect with subscribers when their interest in your brand is highest.

Use this opportunity to introduce your brand, share expectations, and encourage subscribers to take their first action.

Before you begin

Before you send any messages from a WhatsApp welcome flow, make sure you have:

  • Set up WhatsApp in Klaviyo
  • Optional: Created a coupon
Why do I need to set up WhatsApp?

You must enable WhatsApp in Klaviyo before adding WhatsApp messages to your flows. Otherwise, you’ll be prompted to complete setup while building the flow.

Why should I create a coupon?

When someone signs up for WhatsApp marketing, they give you a direct communication channel.

Build a WhatsApp welcome flow from scratch

Use these steps to create a simple 1-message WhatsApp welcome flow.

  1. Click Flows.
  2. Click Create Flow.
  3. Click Create From Scratch.
  4. Name the flow (e.g., WhatsApp-only welcome).
  5. Click Create Flow.
  6. Choose Metric as the trigger type.
  7. Click the trigger dropdown.
  8. Select Subscribed to WhatsApp Marketing.
  9. Click Flow Filters.
  10. Click Add a Flow Filter.
  11. Select Has not been in this flow.
  12. Leave the second dropdown as at any time.
  13. Click Save.
  14. Click Done.
  15. Drag a WhatsApp message after the trigger.
  16. Click Edit in the sidebar.
  17. Add your message content. Keep it short and direct.
  18. Click Save Content.
  19. Click Review and Turn On.

Personalize your WhatsApp welcome flow

Start with a simple flow, then personalize it as your subscriber list grows.

Consider splitting your flow based on:

  • Repeat vs. first-time buyers
  • Purchase history
  • Profile or custom properties

WhatsApp welcome flow best practices

Keep these best practices in mind:

  • Limit your welcome flow to 1–3 messages.
  • Keep messages concise and action-oriented.
  • Introduce your brand and set expectations clearly.

If needed, review your quiet hours settings to ensure your first message sends at the right time.
