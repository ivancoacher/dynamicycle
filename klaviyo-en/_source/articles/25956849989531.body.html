Learn how to change the organization and personal language settings for your Klaviyo account. Changing the language also updates the regional formatting (e.g., for numbers and dates).

Understanding organization language settings vs. personal language settings

Each Klaviyo account has 2 language settings:

  1. Organization language setting
      • Only account Owners and Admins can update this setting.
      • This setting applies to the account as a whole and is used for administrative communications (e.g., billing messages).
      • The account's organization setting does not affect the language you see within the Klaviyo application.
  2. Personal language setting
    • Each individual user within a Klaviyo account can set their personal language setting.
    • This setting determines the language you see in Klaviyo. It applies to all accounts you access and persists across browser sessions.
    • Emails (besides billing messages) sent from Klaviyo to individual recipients will be sent in their personal language selection.

When adding a new user to an account, Owners and Admins can select their personal language preference. This will be used in the invitation and onboarding process, although the new user can modify their personal language settings later, if needed.

Changing your Language and regional format settings will not automatically change your Time zone or Account currency settings. For instance, if you change from English US to Français, you’ll see the number $123, 345. 89 formatted as 123456, 89$; however, the default currency will not change from the US dollar to the Euro. Both Timezone and Account currency will remain in your account’s default selection, unless adjusted separately on the Organization settings page.

This guide will cover how to change the organization and personal language settings for an account in Klaviyo. For instructions on how to change the language for a Portfolio, see Understanding your portfolio settings.

Change your organization language setting

Only account Owners and Admins can configure the organization language setting. To do so:

  1. Click on your organization name in the bottom left corner of Klaviyo.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Organization.
  3. Scroll down to the Organization language and regional format section.
  4. Click into the Organization language dropdown and select a language. At this time, Klaviyo supports the following languages:
      • English
      • French (Français)
      • German (Deutsch)
      • Italian (Italiano)
      • Korean (한국어)
      • Portuguese (Português)
      • Spanish (Spain) - (Español (España))
      • Spanish (Mexico) - (Español (México))
      • Swedish (Svenska)
      • Dutch (Nederlands)
      • Polish (Polski)
  5. Click Save to confirm the change.

Change your personal language setting

To update your personal language setting:

  1. Click on your organization name in the bottom left corner.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Personal.
  3. Scroll to the Personal language and regional format section and click the Language dropdown.
  4. Select your preferred language from the list. Note that at this time, Klaviyo supports the following languages:
      • English
      • French (Français)
      • German (Deutsch)
      • Italian (Italiano)
      • Korean (한국어)
      • Portuguese (Português)
      • Spanish (Spain) - (Español (España))
      • Spanish (Mexico) - (Español (México))
      • Swedish (Svenska)
      • Dutch (Nederlands)
      • Polish (Polski)
  5. Select "Show English metric and event names" if you do not wish to translate event and metric names. This might be useful if you are used to the English names of these events or need to reference them in a programmatic manner.
  6. Click Save.

This change will apply immediately across all accounts you access. The Klaviyo Help Center is also available in each of these supported languages for guidance while navigating the platform.

Navigating Klaviyo in a new language

When you set a new personal language, most elements of the Klaviyo platform will translate. However, some will remain in English. Here's what you can expect:

What translates to your personal language setting

  • All pages and page elements within the Klaviyo application (e.g., headings, buttons, subtext, etc.)
  • The subject line assistant
  • Regional formatting for data, including:
    • Date
    • Time
    • Timezone; this only refers to how the timezone is formatted.
    • Number
    • Currency; this only refers to how currency is formatted, such as spacing and separators in currency values (e.g., decimal points or commas).
    • Percentage
  • Account alerts on the Klaviyo Homepage
  • Event and metric name (e.g., Placed Order)

Email support and our Virtual assistant are available for each of the supported languages. Learn how to contact support.

What remains in English

  • Flows and segmentation AI
  • Nearly all pre-built templates within each content library (e.g., campaign templates, flow templates, form templates, etc.). Note that some translated templates are available in French only.
  • Any form or message content initially written in English or another language
    • Note that for any new templates that you create, you will still need to manually customize the content based on language.
  • Dynamic variables (e.g., unsubscribe tags like {% unsubscribe %} )
  • Time zone and Account currency settings will not change automatically when you set a new language. Only how they're formatted adjusts based on the selected language.
