Before you begin

Some countries have requirements when applying for a long code. Click on the subsection below that applies to your country to view the requirements. 

Australia

Australia requires that every business requesting a long code provides certain information. You’ll need to show proof of your business’s:

  • Name
  • ID number (i.e., Australian business number)

A commercial registry (or equivalent documentation) can be used as proof of all of the above. 

 

Belgium

To register a Belgian long code (LC), the business must have a registered business address in Belgium. Only businesses with a Belgian address are eligible to obtain a Belgian LC.

Ireland

Ireland requires that every business requesting a long code provides certain information. You’ll need to show proof of your business’s:

  • Name
  • Business registration 

For the documentation, you can submit proof of business registration from an authorized government authority. Examples include:

  • IE Company Registration Office proof of business registration
  • UK Companies House certificate of incorporation

 

Italy

To obtain a long code in Italy, every business must provide:

  • Business address 
  • Proof of business address 

 

Best practices

Before requesting a long code, let your SMS recipients know that you’ll be changing your number. For details, check out this article on how to alert your subscribers about a change in number.

Long code requests have different approval times depending on the country. In most cases, the long codes are available immediately or, in Australia, Belgium, and Ireland, in 1-3 business days. 

Once your request has been approved, your long code will be automatically assigned to your account. For most countries, your long code will be the default sending number for all messages for the corresponding country. 

 

Request a long code in Klaviyo

If you send to multiple countries, each country must have a separate long code, so you must repeat this process per country.

  1. Navigate to Settings > SMS.
  2. Go to the Sending information section.
  3. Either:
    • Select Add country (if you don't see your country listed), then seach for that country in the resulting modal. 
    • In the line with the flag for your country, click the 3 vertical dots on the right-hand side and click Edit.
  4. In the modal, choose Long code.
    • This is the only option for Belgium. 
  5. Click Next.
  6. If required for your country:
    • Add information about your business. Information entered here must align with your business registration documents. 
    • Upload your supporting documents as proof of business registration. 
    • Select Submit Request. 
  7. Click Exit.

Outcome

If your long code request requires documentation, it will be available in 1 to 3 business days. Otherwise, it will be available immediately. Once available, you can start adding it to your sign-up forms, website, or email banners.
