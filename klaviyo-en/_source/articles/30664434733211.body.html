About partner-created templates

Partner-created templates are created by members of Klaviyo’s tech and agency partner program. These Klaviyo experts design templates that are available to all and free to use from your Klaviyo account. You can identify these templates by the Partner created badge on the template card.

To browse and use partner-created templates:

  1. Navigate to Content > Templates.
  2. Navigate to the Email templates tab.
  3. Select Partner created from the filters menu.
  4. Click any template to see a preview. You can see the partner that created it in the preview modal.
  5. Select Use template to open the template editor and edit the template.

Partner-created template FAQs

Does it cost anything to use partner-created templates?

No.

Who created these templates?

Klaviyo tech and agency partners were selected to create these templates.

I represent an agency. Can we submit a template to add to the library?

Thanks for your interest! Reach out to your Klaviyo partner success manager to express interest in submitting a template.

How can I learn more about the partner that created a template?

You can see the agency name and website for each partner-created template’s creator in the template selector preview modal. Head to their website to get in touch and learn more about their offerings.
