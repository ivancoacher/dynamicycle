Learn how to analyze your total conversions and revenue, including Klaviyo attributed values, via the Conversion overview dashboard. You can also review how many profiles are completing different numbers of conversions and the total revenue they represent. This dashboard enables you to optimize marketing efforts and increase conversion rates by targeting specific segments based on conversion behaviors.

Advanced KDP and Marketing Analytics are not included in Klaviyo’s standard marketing application, and a subscription is required to access the associated functionality.Head to our billing guide to learn about how to purchase these plans.

Before you begin

This dashboard is calculated using event data. Thus, you may see a difference in how other Klaviyo reports or dashboards calculate attributed revenue (e.g., the Home dashboard uses data based on when the message was sent).

Accessing and customizing the dashboard

It is not possible to remove or replace the cards on the Conversion overview dashboard.

  1. If you are an Advanced KDP customer, navigate to Advanced KDP > Intelligence > Customer insights > Conversion overview. Alternatively, if you are a Marketing Analytics customer, navigate to Marketing Analytics > Customer insights > Conversion overview.
  2. Optional: Once inside the dashboard, select the Conversion metric dropdown to adjust the metric used to calculate the dashboard. For example, if you choose Ordered Product, you will see revenue and conversions related to completed purchases. Learn more about tracking message conversion tracking.
  3. Click on the time perioddropdown. Choose from the following options:
      • Last 30 days (default option)
      • Last 90 days
      • Month-to-date
      • Year-to-date
      • Last month
      • Last 3 months
      • Last 12 months
      • Year-to-date
      • Custom
  4. Optional: if you want to narrow your dashboard to show data specific to a certain segment, click on the Segment dropdown. From here, choose the segment you want to evaluate.
    Note that if your time period is before June 21, 2023, you cannot choose a specific segment. Additionally, if you analyze all active profiles and not a specific segment, it will pull in all profiles that were active within your chosen time period.

    Conversions are based on segment membership at the time the conversation event occurred.

    For example, say you have a segment of profiles that have never placed an order. When a profile places their first order, they will exit the segment. However, since the profile was in the non-purchasers segment at the time they placed their order, the revenue is attributed to the original segment at the time of the purchase (i.e., profiles that have never placed an order).

  5. Optional: to change the comparison period, select the Previous period dropdown.
