## You will learn

Learn how to see how automation messages will appear to customers as well as how to test the entire automation without turning it on.

## Before you begin

Please note that the preview can be slightly different than the live experience.

Compared to when automations are turned on, the preview does not:

- Show shortened links
- Catch any typos

## Preview an individual message

Previewing an individual message shows you how it will appear to customers:

1. Click into that message.
2. Select the ****Preview**** tab in the right sidebar.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34175751790875)
3. Review your message.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34175747467803)

## Walk through the automation

With automations, you also have the ability to test the entire experience.

Previewing an automation is simple.

To preview an automation:

1. Navigate to the automation.
2. In the upper right, click the preview button.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34175751792411)
3. Test the automation by typing in responses.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34175751799707)
4. Once you reach a recommendation, you can restart the preview by selecting ****Reset Preview****.
   ![](https://klaviyo.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34175747472667)
